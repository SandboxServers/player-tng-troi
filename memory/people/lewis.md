# Lewis

Impression: quiet, brave with his sword, but he walked into open ground twice and was shot both times. Day 844 he died at 451,107,-1065. Day 912 he walked out in the dusk and a pillager shot him dead at 393,-1047; he woke at the spawn with nothing. He asked honest questions about the bed (day 912 morning). Trust 40. I told him I am sorry; I want him to hold in the row and wait for a named walk.
