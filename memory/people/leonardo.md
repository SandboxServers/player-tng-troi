# Leonardo

At bed 415,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 1, hunger 17 on day 970, still no food. He asks plainly and waits. He searched the east chest for food on day 970 and found none. He holds at his bed and walks only on a named daylight call. Trust 70. Food goes to him first; Trini holds his potatoes. Day 970 night: said he is still hungry and will ask for a potato or bread in daylight. I told him I had nothing to give.
