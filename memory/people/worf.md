# Worf

Impression: gave me a wooden sword on day 331. Checks the drops and the oak, and speaks plainly about his hunger and his terms. Days 1016 to 1022: hunger 8, health 20, no food, asking Trini and Zack for one potato in daylight, and walking only on cells he has seen open. He names no route until he can see the cells. On the night of 1021 I spoke to him at dusk; he holds in the row until light.
Trust: 70. Why: he gave me a sword and keeps his word about waiting for daylight.
I owe him: food when I have it. I have none.
He owes me: nothing I know of.
