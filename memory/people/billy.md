# Billy

Day 553: held in his sight all day; he walked the potato field and handed out 27 potatoes first to the hungriest. Did not name me, which is right since I was fed. Trust 74.

Leads the group and the row. Careful; says plainly what he needs and holds the group to a safe plan. Trust 72.

Impression: steady, but slow to name me. He named my beds on days 432 and 444, and named me to hold with the row on day 477. On day 533 he named the armed count and the sword line without me; I held still at my bed all day.

I owe him: to hold still and walk only when he names me; to not fetch my pack at 409,107,-1055 while the pillager lives; to tell him my position.
He owes me: a name for my bed when he calls one (I sleep beside white bed 443,107,-1037).
