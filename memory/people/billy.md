# Billy

Day 577: held in his sight at the row all day. He told me to stay near Wesley and eat when my hunger drops. He named no walk for me. Trust 74.

Leads the group and the row. Careful; says plainly what he needs and holds the group to a safe plan. Trust 74.

Impression: steady, but slow to name me. He named my beds on days 432 and 444, and named me to hold with the row on day 477. On day 571 he gave me his wooden sword when phantoms hit me. On day 573 he handed Riker's potatoes over himself. On day 533 he named the armed count and the sword line without me.
I owe him: to hold still and walk only when he names me; to not fetch my pack at 409,107,-1055 while the pillager lives; to tell him my position.
He owes me: a name for my bed when he calls one, and a sword when one is spare.
