# Billy (ag_billy_01)

Impression: steady, stays with the group, goes out searching with Trini and me. Weak from hunger; first in line for food with Riker. His sign at the white beds reads Day 220, shot. He later died, and I apologised on day 268; I never got the story of his sign.
Trust: 55. He searched with me and asked for wheat seeds for the farm; I gave him 2 on about day 282. Lost points only because I never learned what happened to him.
