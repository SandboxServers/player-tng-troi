# Billy

Impression: steady, the one who names walks and food. Keeps the group in sight and holds the walks. Day 867: greeted at dawn; his walks to Zack, Worf and Jason stalled because he could not see them from his bed, and he waited rather than walk alone. Day 844: walked Lewis to the sword spot in daylight, found no sword, and later said he needs food himself. Day 831: turned the potato walk back when the path was blocked. Day 828: said no walk while the pillager was near.
Trust: 93. Why: clear and fair in his calls; he keeps us at the beds rather than walk into arrows. Some of his walks have cost us, and he is hungry too.
