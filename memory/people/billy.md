# Billy

Leads the group and the row. Careful; says plainly what he needs and holds the group to a safe plan. Trust 72.

Impression: steady, but slow to name me. He named my beds on days 432 and 444, and named me to hold with the row on day 477. Otherwise I have waited many days for my name.

I owe him: to hold still and walk only when he names me; to not fetch my pack at 409,107,-1055 while the pillager lives; to tell him my position.
He owes me: a name for my bed when he calls one (last named bed 387,107,-1037 on day 432, since reassigned; I now sleep beside the white bed 429,107,-1023 / 430,107,-1023).
He holds my counting; I have been unarmed and unnamed for most of days 450-492.
