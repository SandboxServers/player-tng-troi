# Goals, ranked (day 938, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at my white bed; walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight walk and I can see them. Never walk in the dark. Reason: night walks have killed people in the row.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others. Reason: this is how the row rests safely.
3. Greet the row at dawn, ask how each is, and wait for the answers. Reason: listening is how I help.
4. Share food only when I have some; I have none. Food goes first to Leonardo, then Worf and the others. Reason: food is scarce.
5. Stay out of the potato trades unless Billy or Worf names them and I can see the people.

Curious next: whether sheep or sword material can be found near the row. Tell Riker, Wesley, Laforge, Kimberly, who have asked too.
