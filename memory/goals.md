# Goals, ranked (day 968, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at my white bed; walk only on a daylight route named by Billy or Worf, with them in sight. Never walk in the dark.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others.
3. At first light, greet the row, ask how each is, and wait for answers. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) first, then Worf (hunger 9), Beverly and John.
4. Share food only when I have some. I have none now. Food goes first to Leonardo, then Worf and the others.
5. Stay out of the potato trades unless Billy or Worf names them and I can see the people.

Curious next: whether open air past the wall can be found near the row. Tell Billy, Wesley and Kimberly.
