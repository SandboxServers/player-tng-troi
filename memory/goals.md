# Goals, ranked (day 877, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at the beds; walk only when Billy names it and I can see him. Never walk in the dark.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others.
3. Greet the row at dawn, ask how each is, and wait for the answer.
4. Eat only at hunger 10 or below, from food Billy hands out. Give food first to Leonardo, then Worf, then Beverly if she is weakest.
5. Walk to the potato patch only when Billy's group is in sight and names it.

Reasons: the row keeps us alive. Night walks killed Lewis. Food is the scarce thing, and Leonardo, Worf and Beverly are the weakest.
