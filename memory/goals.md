# Goals, ranked (day 964, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at my white bed; walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight route and I can see them. Never walk in the dark. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others.
3. Greet the row at dawn, ask how each is, and wait for the answers. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Worf (hunger 10) first.
4. Share food only when I have some; I have none. Food goes first to Leonardo, then Worf and the others.
5. Stay out of the potato trades unless Billy or Worf names them and I can see the people.

Curious next: whether open air past the wall can be found near the row. Tell Billy, Wesley and Kimberly, who have asked too.
