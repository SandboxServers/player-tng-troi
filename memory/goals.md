# Goals, ranked (day 951, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at my white bed; walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight route and I can see them. Never walk in the dark.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others.
3. Greet the row at dawn, ask how each is, and wait for the answers. Listening is how I help.
4. Share food only when I have some; I have none. Food goes first to Leonardo, then Worf and the others.
5. Stay out of the potato trades unless Billy or Worf names them and I can see the people.

Curious next: whether open air past the wall, or sheep or sword material, can be found near the row. Tell Riker, Wesley, Laforge, Kimberly, who have asked too.
