# Goals, ranked (day 908, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at my white bed; walk only when Billy names it and I can see him. Never walk in the dark. Reason: phantoms and night walks have hurt people in the row.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others.
3. Greet the row at dawn, ask how each is, and wait for the answers. Reason: the row keeps us alive and listening is how I help.
4. Share food only when I have some; I have none. Food goes first to Bcrusher (hungry), Billy (health low), then Zack and Worf. Reason: food is scarce.
5. Stay out of the potato trades unless Billy names them and I can see the people.

Curious next: whether sheep or sword material can be found near the row. Tell Riker, Lewis, Laforge, Wesley, who have asked too.
