# Goals, ranked (day 912, night)

1. Stay alive. Hold at my white bed; walk only when Billy or an armed person (Worf) names it and I can see them. Pillagers shoot on open ground, so no walks past the open west or east. Never walk in the dark. Reason: pillagers and night walks have killed and hurt people in the row.
2. Sleep each night beside a bed, in sight of others.
3. Greet the row at dawn, ask how each is, and wait for the answers. Reason: the row keeps us alive and listening is how I help.
4. Share food only when I have some; I have none. Food goes first to Leonardo (health 1), Worf (hunger 12), then Zack and Jason. Reason: food is scarce.
5. Stay out of the potato trades unless Billy or Worf names them and I can see the people.

Curious next: whether sheep or sword material can be found near the row, and whether beds take a sleeper only at night. Tell Riker, Wesley, Laforge, Kimberly, who have asked too.
