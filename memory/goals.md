# Goals, day 349 (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group at the bed row and hold for the lowest. Reason: they are my people, and I have no food to give.
2. Join Billy's cow walk only when he calls it and the group is together. Reason: no one walks alone.
3. Keep myself fed; find food through trade or the cows, and sleep in a white bed at night (try 401,-1037 first). Reason: I have no food of my own.
4. Ask Clark whether the wall closes the beds. Reason: I want to sleep inside it.
5. Keep the daily log. Reason: the next day depends on it.
