# Goals, ranked (day 773)

1. Stay alive. Pillagers are west and south-west. Do not walk unless Billy names a walk.
2. Sleep in sight of others. The bed at 443,107,-1023 worked at dusk 773 with Trini and Lewis near. Try it again at each dusk, beside the bed, never alone.
3. Eat at hunger 10 or below. Keep my one potato for that, and call Billy first.
4. Stay in Billy's row, in sight of others, and answer whoever speaks to me.
5. Ask Data how he is when he is in sight. He has not answered yet.
6. Recover my dropped pack at 409,107,-1055 only when the pillager is dead and the group is with me.

Reasons: sleep is still urgent, and the bed now works in company. Walking alone is deadly, so I hold in the row until Billy names a walk.
