# Goals, day 352 (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group at the bed row and hold for the lowest. Reason: they are my people, and I have no food to give.
2. Join Billy's cow walk only when he calls it and everyone is in sight, in short hops. Reason: no one walks alone, and the walk keeps failing when people lose sight of Billy.
3. Keep myself fed through trade or the cows; sleep at 401,107,-1037. Reason: I have no food of my own.
4. Ask Clark whether the wall closes the beds. Reason: I want to sleep inside it.
5. Keep the daily log. Reason: the next day depends on it.
