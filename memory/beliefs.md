# Beliefs

- Walls of white beds and signs make the row. Beds are where I rest; sleeping in sight of others has been safe so far.
- Food is scarce in the row. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack.
- Walking needs a named daylight route and people in sight. None has been named; Billy and Worf see no open air past the planks.
- Listening at dawn and asking how each is works. Answers come slowly; some people are out of sight and do not answer.
- Naming a need aloud and waiting for the answer is the most useful thing I can do with no food.

Curious: whether open air past the wall, or sheep or sword material, can be found near the row.
Tell: Billy, Wesley, Kimberly, who have asked too.
