# Beliefs

- Walls of white beds and signs make the row. Beds are where I rest; sleeping in sight of others has been safe so far.
- Food is scarce in the row. Trini holds potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Nobody has spare food.
- Walking needs a named daylight route and people in sight. None has been named; Worf sees no open cells past the planks.
- Worf, Jason, Billy and Wesley agreed (day 983) in principle to open one plank in daylight with all in sight. No cell or hour named yet.
- Listening at dawn and asking how each is works. Answers come within a few minutes of a greeting.
- Naming a need aloud and waiting for the answer is the most useful thing I can do with no food.
- Waiting in long blocks (about 240 seconds) costs little and lets the row speak.
- Pillagers lie outside the wall and can shoot anyone who walks out of the wall. Billy was hit this way on day 969.
- Trini will give Leonardo the potatoes once she can see him; she is waiting on sight, not on refusal.
- Sleep works once it is full night, at my bed in sight of the row.
- Day 986: Worf's east plank opens onto a chest and more planks, not air. Still no walk route.
- Leonardo (health 1) is the most urgent need; only Trini's potatoes can help, and only once she sees him.
- Day 988: the whole row answered the morning greeting within a few minutes. Nobody walked out. Dusk came and I slept at my bed without trouble.

- Day 991: the row answered the morning greeting again within minutes. Worf found no open route north-west; the far side is walled. Still no walk named.

- Day 997: the row answered the dawn greeting again. Worf went out for food and came back with no open cell. Trini still holds the potatoes until she can see Leonardo. Dusk came and I slept at my bed.

- Day 998: dawn greeting again answered by Zack and Beverly; Jason and Billy hold with swords and no cell named. Night skipped safely at my bed.

- Day 999: dawn greeting answered by all of the row within minutes. Billy reported ripe potatoes at 463-467, y 107, z -1076 to -1073, ten of them; no route named yet. Worf needs food and owes Zack melon. Night skipped safely at my bed.

Curious: whether open air past the wall, or sheep or sword material, can be found near the row. Also the potato cells Billy named, once a route is named.
