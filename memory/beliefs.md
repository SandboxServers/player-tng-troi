# Beliefs

What worked:
- Oak logs crafted into oak planks (4 planks from my logs).
- Crafting a crafting table from planks, then a wooden hoe at it; a hoe lets you till the ground for wheat.
- Breaking short grass gives wheat seeds, about 1 in 5 to 10 breaks. Seeds are given to whoever farms (Wesley, Kimberly, Riker).
- Sleep at a free white bed at night when no golem, creeper or phantom is within about 8 blocks. Night 276 and night 281 worked at (-270/-271,67,318).
- Asking a person by name for food or materials, and giving food to the weakest in reach first.
- A pig hunt north, near (-238,68,227), gave two porkchops on day 251. Cows near (-228,68,209) gave beef around day 276. Both were reached with the group.

What did not work:
- A gather with no reason given can fail; a named request to a person worked better.
- Searching the west grass, the wheat field and the oak leaves for apples or animals found nothing (days 229 to 260). Struck out: "Apples have been found on the west grass" is false.
- Fishing the salmon lake without a rod: salmon stay in deep water out of reach, and a drowned hit me in the water (day 270).
- Sleep at the beds at dusk is only sometimes true. It fails when beds are full, when a golem or creeper is close, or when phantoms are in range. Struck out: "a bed at dusk always works" is false.
- Gives by name often landed on the wrong person (porkchop to Riker, wheat to Kimberly, chicken to Kimberly). Check the person is in reach before giving.
- Tilling a grass patch by the beds failed; the spot had no tilled dirt to plant in.

Why it matters:
- Food is the bottleneck. No steady food source has been found since day 229, so every other plan waits on it.
- Iron golems roam the south and do not attack, but they close in at dusk and block sleep.
- Signs at the beds mark deaths: Picard and Tommy, Day 220, shot. Billy's sign reads Day 220, shot. Billy also died; I wrote him an apology on day 268, and his death is in the pending journal.
- A trader near the west edge (day 269) wanted bread or meat; I had only logs and leather, so no trade happened.
