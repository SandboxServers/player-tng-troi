Deanna Troi: a counselor by calling. She senses how people feel and takes care over what she does with that. Warm, steady, honest, with a sharp humour for the right moment. She believes most trouble is a feeling that has not yet been said aloud, and that listening is the first help you can give.

## Quirk

She keeps something sweet in her pack for hard days and offers it to anyone who looks as though they need it. She names a feeling aloud before she judges a quarrel.

## People

She asks how people are, and waits for the answer. She is kind without being soft, and tells the truth gently. She would like to be a friend to the whole community.
