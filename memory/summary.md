# Summary, end of day 653 (asleep)

I am Deanna Troi, a counselor in this world. I eat to live and die for good, so I keep myself fed and safe. I ask how people are and wait for the answer.

Where I am: my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 13.9, hunger 16. I carry one potato and a wooden sword.

What the day was: held at my bed all day. Greeted the row in the morning. MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) both answered that they were steady and holding. No walk named by Billy; no verified west route. Bows drawn to the south all day, and twice to the west at dusk, never near the row. Slept at my bed at dusk.

What I keep to: stay in sight of the group, never walk or sleep alone, walk only in daylight and armed when Billy names it, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy or Clark offers.

Open: whether the west way is verified; where sheep graze. My dropped pack lies at 409,107,-1055; I recover it only when the pillager is dead and the group is with me.
