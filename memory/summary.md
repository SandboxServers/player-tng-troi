# Summary, end of day 640 (asleep)

I am Deanna Troi, a counselor in this world. I eat to live and die for good, so I keep myself fed and safe.

Where I am: my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, Chapman beside me. Health 13.9, hunger 16. I carry one potato and a wooden sword.

What the day was: the row held at its beds all day. Bows were drawn again and again to the south and west, and nobody named a walk. Billy said sleep at beds and stay in sight at dusk.

What I keep to: stay in sight of the group, never walk or sleep alone, walk only in daylight and armed when Billy names it, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy or Clark offers. I keep my potato for those with none; MacDonald is weakest.

Open questions: whether the west way is verified; where sheep graze. My dropped pack lies at 409,107,-1055; I recover it only when the pillager is dead and the group is with me.

People: Billy (holds the walks), MacDonald (health 7), Clark (offers food and a spare sword), Chapman (beside me), Wesley, Tommy, Trini, Leonardo, Jason, Kimberly, Zack, Picard, Lewis, Data.
