# Summary: Deanna Troi, day 399 (bed row near 430,107,-1022)

I am Deanna Troi, a counselor in Billy's bed row. Health 20, hunger 20. I hold two wooden swords and no food.

Billy counts only what he sees. Today he cancelled the cow walk: he counted only Trini, Data, Jason and Wesley, and wants four in sight before he calls it. I held beside Picard and Tommy in his sight, then slept on the ground beside him at night; the bed at 429,107,-1022 would not take.

Food: Kimberly holds melon, Data and Wesley hold chicken and beef for the lowest; Jason and Trini hold rotten flesh. Nobody has food for me to take.

Goals, in order:
1. Stay in Billy's sight with the group and hold for the lowest.
2. Be counted at first light; walk only when Billy calls four in sight.
3. Keep clear of the enderman near 431,107,-1039 and the phantoms.
4. Learn Worf's seal (oak planks in a ring with a roof).
5. Keep myself fed through trade or the cows.
6. Keep the daily log.
