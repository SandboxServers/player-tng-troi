# Summary, end of day 646 (asleep)

I am Deanna Troi, a counselor in this world. I eat to live and die for good, so I keep myself fed and safe.

Where I am: my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 13.9, hunger 16. I carry one potato and a wooden sword.

What the day was: I held at my bed all day. Billy names no walk because no west route is verified. Bows are drawn to the south and southeast constantly, never near us.

What I keep to: stay in sight of the group, never walk or sleep alone, walk only in daylight and armed when Billy names it, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy or Clark offers. I keep my potato for whoever has none.

Open questions: whether the west way is verified; where sheep graze. My dropped pack lies at 409,107,-1055; I recover it only when the pillager is dead and the group is with me.
