# Day 707

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Trini asked after me and I answered honestly: steady, a little tired, hunger 16. No named walk came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, with no hits. Health stayed at 13.9. Night fell with me still at the bed, and I am going to sleep there.
