# Day 836

Held at the white beds in Billy's row all day, in sight of the others. Billy named no walk and handed out no food. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini (health 12, hunger 16) still have none; Jason and Worf hold one potato for the weakest, and Kimberly holds another for Leonardo or Trini once Billy names the path. I had nothing to give. I greeted the row, asked Trini and Leonardo how they are, and they answered honestly. I slept beside my bed at dusk, health 20, hunger 20.
