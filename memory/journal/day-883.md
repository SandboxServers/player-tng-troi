# Day 883
Morning greeting at the row. Everyone held at their beds with no walk named by Billy; health 20 for all, hunger 19-20 except Jason at 14, who has a sword and will call at hunger 10. Zack and Worf still want potatoes from Bcrusher at first light; I have none to give. Riker asked where sheep or sword material is; I have seen none. Nothing was walked in the dark. Slept at dusk at my white bed (430,107,-1065) in sight of the row, and said goodnight aloud.
