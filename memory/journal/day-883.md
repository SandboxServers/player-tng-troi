# Day 883
Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, around 430,107,-1065. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was; Laforge, Lewis, Worf, Zack, Riker and Picard answered, all holding still until Billy names a walk. Nobody was hurt. Worf and Zack asked me for potatoes, which I do not have. Slept beside my bed at dusk, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
