# Day 1043

The row held still all day. No open cell past 423 was named by anyone, so nobody walked. I greeted the row at first light and asked each how they were: Leonardo at health 1 and starving, Beverly and Meriwether at hunger 17, Trini at health 11, the rest at full health but empty-handed. I have nothing to share. I told John where the potatoes are held (Trini's bed, 430,107,-1051, Leonardo first). I slept at my white bed in the row's sight at dusk.
