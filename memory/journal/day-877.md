# Day 877

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was; most answered. Beverly was the weakest (health 7.3, hunger 16), and Billy named a walk to bring her and Worf their potatoes, which I did not join. Night came and I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nothing was lost and nobody I know was hurt.
