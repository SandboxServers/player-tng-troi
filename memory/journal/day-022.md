# Day 1022

Held at my white bed through dusk, dawn and night, in sight of the row. No food in my pack, none given or received. Greeted the row at first light and asked each how they are; all answered and all are holding. Leonardo is at health 1 and asks for any potato or bread; I have nothing to give him. Worf is at hunger 8 and Trini holds two potatoes for him. No open cell past 423 at z -1051 has been seen by anyone, so no walk was taken. I slept at my bed at night in sight of others.
