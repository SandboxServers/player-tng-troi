# Day 699

The row held all day at the beds in Billy's sight. No walk was named, and no verified route west was found. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, with no hits. I greeted the row at each waking and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Trini asked after me and MacDonald; I told her I am well enough (health 14, hunger 16) and keep my potato until she calls at hunger 10. Kimberly was refused the far bed at 443,107,-1022, which the row now knows. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 at dusk.
