# Day 882

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, at 430,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning and asked each of the row how they were; Lewis, Riker, Laforge and Picard all answered, all at full health. Worf and Zack went toward Bcrusher's potato patch on Billy's word, got blocked at the table, and held in sight without anyone being hurt. No walk was named for me, so I stayed put. Riker asked about sheep and sword material; I had seen none. Slept beside my bed in sight of the row at nightfall. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt.
