# Day 849

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo and Trini how they are. Leonardo (10, hunger 17) and Trini (12, hunger 16) still have no food; Billy and Beverly hold 10 potatoes for them, and Worf holds one for Leonardo. I have no food to give. No walk was named, so I stayed. A bed use failed at night, and I slept beside the bed. Nothing hurt me. Lesson: the row works when we wait for Billy's word together.
