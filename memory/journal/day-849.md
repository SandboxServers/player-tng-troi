# Day 849

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Leonardo and Trini still have no food; Billy and Beverly hold 10 potatoes for them, and Worf holds one for Leonardo. I greeted the row, said goodnight at the bed, and stayed in sight of others. The night sleep attempt returned only events, not a full rest, so I will check again at dawn.
