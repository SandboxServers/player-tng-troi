# Day 1126

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing was gained: no food, no route. Kimberly (health 20), MacDonald (health 20) and Beverly (health 9, hunger 17) each answered when I asked how they were, and each is still waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, and no one walked. I did not leave the beds. Slept safely at the bed beside the row.
