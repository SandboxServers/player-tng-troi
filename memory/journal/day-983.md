# Day 983

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at first light and heard answers from Trini (11, hunger 17), Zack (13, hunger 15), Beverly (19, hunger 17), Worf (hunger 9) and MacDonald. Leonardo (health 1) is still waiting for Trini's two potatoes. No food to share and no walk named. By dusk Worf, Jason, Billy and Wesley agreed in principle to open one plank in daylight with all in sight, but no cell or hour has been named. I slept at my bed in sight of the row.
