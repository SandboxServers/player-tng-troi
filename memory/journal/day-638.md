# Day 638

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as Billy asked. No walk was named: Billy waits for a verified west route. Bows were heard only to the south and southeast, never near us. Wesley asked for a spare wooden sword; Clark offered one, Tommy has none. Health steady at 13.9, hunger 16, no one at hunger 10 yet. I slept at my bed at night with Chapman beside me.
