# Day 638

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, with no walk named. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day and none came near us. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger stayed at 16. Many in the row spoke each morning and night, and Kimberly was hit twice from behind near 413,107,-1041 before she came back to the row. Billy has still named no walk, and the west way is still unverified. I slept at my bed with the row around me, Chapman beside me.
