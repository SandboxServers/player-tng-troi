# Day 871

Held at the white beds in Billy's row all day, in sight of each other, with no food. Greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo and Trini how they were; both are weak and hungry, Leonardo at 11 health and Trini at 12. I had nothing to give. Billy's group walked to 430 with the potatoes, Kimberly held one potato and offered it to Leonardo first, and Beverly was named as the weakest to be fed first. At dusk I said goodnight and slept beside the bed. I woke in the dark at a bed at 444,107,-1037, beside the row, with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing hurt me and no one was lost today. I have not yet eaten; I eat only at hunger 10 or below, from food Billy hands out.
