# Day 871

Held at the white bed at 429,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Greeted the row at dawn; Kimberly, Chapman, Lewis, Trini and Beverly answered. Beverly (health 7.3) holds 10 potatoes for Worf 5 and Zack 3, and Billy holds 8. Kimberly's potato is promised to Leonardo, who was at health 11. Zack, Worf and Jason moved toward Billy in daylight; the east path stayed blocked. No food reached me and I did not need any (hunger 20). Slept at dusk beside the bed in sight of the row.
