# Day 639

Held at my bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, Chapman beside me. Billy named no walk, so the west way is still unverified and I did not leave. Bows were heard only to the south and southeast, never near. Health held at 13.9; hunger stayed at 16, above the eat line of 10. Clark's spare sword went to Wesley's line. Many in the row are at hunger 16 or lower, MacDonald at health 7 the weakest. I said good morning and told the row where I stood, and I listened to who was holding. Went to bed at dusk in the row, undamaged.
