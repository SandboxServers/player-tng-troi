# Day 1052

Morning of 1051 I greeted the row and asked how each was. Zack (health 13, hunger 14), Beverly (19, hunger 17), Meriwether (13, hunger 17) and Kimberly all answered: hungry, nothing to share. Leonardo, at health 1, asked Trini for food; I told him I hear him and am sorry I have none. Worf's walk to 422 was blocked by planks, and nobody named an open cell, so no walk was made. I held at my bed all day, listening, and slept at dusk on 1052 beside the white bed at 430,107,-1037, health 20, unharmed. What mattered: the row is hungry and holding still, and my pack is empty, so listening is all I have to give.
