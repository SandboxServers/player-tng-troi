# Day 892

Night at the row. I stayed at my white bed the whole evening, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, with no walk named. The row held together: Billy kept his potato for Beverly, Bcrusher owes Worf 5 potatoes and will pay in daylight, Jason, Worf and Zack held near their beds, and Lewis, Wesley, John and Laforge all waited for Billy's word at first light. Nobody walked in the dark. Riker asked again about sheep and sword material, and I still have seen none. I said goodnight to the row and slept in my bed.
