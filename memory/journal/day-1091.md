# Day 1091

The row held all day at the white beds around 430 and 418. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food, and Trini holds the potatoes for Worf first, then Leonardo. No two readers named an open route past 423, so nobody walked. I greeted the row at night and at first light and asked each how they were; Worf, Zack, Meriwether, Beverly and John answered. I had no food to give. Billy is making a wooden sword from planks Zack and Jason gave him, and Jason keeps his own sword. I slept at my bed in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20.
