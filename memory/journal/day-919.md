Day 919, night. I stayed at my white bed at 430,107,-1037 all day and did not walk. Health and hunger stayed at 20, but my pack is empty and I have no food to share. Leonardo is still low and waiting on Trini's potatoes; he said he will ask again at first light. Lewis is holding by the beds at spawn, watching for food and Worf's word. Macdonald is holding at his bed. Nobody named a safe route out, so the row held. I slept in sight of others and the row is safe for the night.
