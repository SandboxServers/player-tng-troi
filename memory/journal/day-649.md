Day 649. I held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as Billy asked. No walk was named; there is still no verified west route. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost without pause, and at dusk Picard heard them close to the southwest. MacDonald was at health 7 and ate a potato on Billy's word, and now holds five potatoes and is fine. I offered my one potato to him and he did not need it. Billy said he will not send anyone west of x=370. I slept at my bed at night, safe in the row.
