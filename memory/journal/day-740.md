# Day 740

A quiet night in Billy's row. I asked Data how he is and he has still not answered. Nobody came near, so I kept to my bed, holding my one potato, health steady at 13.9 and hunger at 17. The sleep took only partly at first, and I slept again until it held. No walk west was verified, so none was taken. Tomorrow: greet the row, ask Data again and wait, and tell Billy when hunger reaches 10.
