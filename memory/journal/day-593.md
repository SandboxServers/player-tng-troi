# Day 593

Night at the row, clear and dim. Health 13.9, hunger 16, eight potatoes and a wooden sword. No walk was named, and the west route is still unverified. Billy could not get into his bed and held in sight of the row. Clark, Laforge and Tommy all said goodnight in sight of the row. I wrote that I hold at my bed and will call Billy at hunger 10. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1051 and the sleep worked. Nothing else happened today.
