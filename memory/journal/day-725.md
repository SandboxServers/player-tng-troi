# Day 725

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Greeted the row at waking and asked Data and MacDonald by name how they feel; neither has answered. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 13.9, steady. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day with no hits. Billy ended the attempt to find a route past x=370 at Wesley's request. Nothing new was learned about the land. Went to bed at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1023, in sight of the row.
