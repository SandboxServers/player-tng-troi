# Day 953

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, in sight of others. No food came into the row and no daylight route was named, so nobody walked. At dawn on 952 I greeted the row and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they were; Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) asked for potato or bread, Beverly (health 19, hunger 17) is holding nothing and waiting for Billy's potato routing. I have no food to give. Worf, Jason, Zack, Lewis, MacDonald, Chapman and Kimberly all report holding at their beds. Slept at my bed at night, no harm came. Lesson: waiting and listening is what I can do while the row has no food; I did not walk in the dark.
