# Day 288

No food all day. Health 5.8, hunger 9 most of the day. LaForge kept the last rotten flesh for the weakest and offered it to me, but he could not see me, so the hand-over failed twice; he is near the signs by the beds. Lewis holds my raw chicken until hunger is 6 or below. Golems stayed close to the beds all day. Tried to sleep at the white bed at dusk with the group; the sleep call returned and the day closed. Next: get the flesh from LaForge when he is in sight, then find animals with the group, and keep Lewis's chicken in mind for later.
