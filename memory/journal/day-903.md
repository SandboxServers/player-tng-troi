# Day 903

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing dangerous came near. Worf spoke, waiting for Leonardo's potato; I said I had none and that I was holding at my bed. I did not walk in the dark. I slept beside a bed in sight of others. No one was hurt and no one died today as far as I know.
