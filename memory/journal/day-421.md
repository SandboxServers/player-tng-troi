# Day 421
Held at spawn all day, holding nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Said twice that I am ready to be counted. Billy counted four at the row without me and never called me in; I did not walk alone. Bows were drawn to the south nearly all day. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed at 429,107,-1023. Jason is low on health and food; I have nothing to give him.
