# Day 421
Held at spawn all day, holding nothing, health and hunger full. Told Billy twice that I am ready to be counted and will walk only on his call. Billy's group of six counted at the 401 beds at dusk and slept there; he did not call me. Bows were drawn to the south all day, so I stayed put. Wesley slept alone at the row bed. I slept in my bed at spawn for the night.
