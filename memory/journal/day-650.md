# Day 650

I held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as I had planned. Bows were drawn to the south and west, never near me. No walk was named, and there is still no verified west route. I kept my potato for whoever has none. At night I said goodnight to the row, Tommy among them, and slept at my bed at 430,107,-1023 with health 13.9 and hunger 16. Nothing new was learned today about sheep or the west way; both remain open.
