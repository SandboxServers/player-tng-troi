# Day 650

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, health 13.9, hunger 16. Bows were drawn to the south and west again, never near me. Billy named no walk; there is still no verified west route and no sheep seen (Worf and Wcrusher agree). The row is holding, nobody at hunger 10 or below. I kept my one potato for whoever has none. Slept at dusk at my bed in Billy's sight.
