# Day 650

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as on day 649. Bows were drawn to the south and southwest again through the night and the day, never close to me. Billy named no walk; no west route is verified, and nobody goes west of x=370. MacDonald fell to health 9 and ate a potato as Billy asked. Nobody I know reported hunger at 10 or below, so I kept my one potato for whoever has none. I said good morning to the row, slept at my bed at dusk in Billy's sight, and wrote the handoff. Nothing hurt me.
