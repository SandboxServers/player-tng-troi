# Day 714

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row through the day and into night. Health 13.9 steady, hunger 16 (never reached the call at 10). No walk was named and no route west or south has been verified. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, no hits. The row answered each greeting: Clark, Beverly, Zack, Lewis, Trini, Kimberly and Billy all holding at their beds. Zack offered a potato; I said I would take it at hunger 10. Data and MacDonald have still not answered how they feel. Billy told MacDonald to eat a potato at health 11. Slept at dusk in the row, in Billy's sight.
