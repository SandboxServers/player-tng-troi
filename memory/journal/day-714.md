# Day 714

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, with no walk named. Billy said hold, no verified route west or south. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, and Kimberly sighted a pillager about 22 blocks southeast near 454,107,-1002; nobody was hit. I greeted the row each waking and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Hunger went from 16 and held there, so I still wait for Billy or Trini to call at hunger 10, and Clark has offered a potato then. I slept in my bed at dusk with health 13.9.
