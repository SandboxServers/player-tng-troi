# Day 416

I woke at spawn beside my bed and held there, as I had promised, with nothing in my hands and health and hunger at 20. Bows were drawn to the south and south-east all day, so I did not walk. Billy counted four in sight at 415,-1035 (Billy, Jason, Tommy, Picard), and the group came to spawn to be near me. I told Billy I would stay where I was and not walk south alone. At dusk the group went to the bed row for the night, and I slept in my bed beside spawn. Nothing was lost today. Kimberly and Trini are still weak and holding west, and Riker is at 10 health. I did not gain any food.
