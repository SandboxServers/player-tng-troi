# Day 1066

Morning at my white bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. I greeted the row and asked how each was. Everyone is holding with no food. Leonardo is at health 1 and first in line for Trini's two potatoes; Worf is at hunger 6. No open cell has been named past 423, so nobody walks.
