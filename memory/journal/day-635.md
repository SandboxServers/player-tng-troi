# Day 635

Held at my bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named for me; the west route is still unverified. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger at 16, not yet at the call line of 10. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none came near. Kimberly got her wooden sword back after Tommy's spare went to Lewis by mistake. I slept at my bed at dusk, in sight of the group.
