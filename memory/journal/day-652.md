# Day 652

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, and Billy says nobody goes west of x=370 since no west route is verified. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never close to the row. I greeted the row at dawn and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel; both are still holding (MacDonald health 11, Data 13). Kimberly reported health 15, hunger 17. Nobody needed food. I still carry my one potato for whoever has none, and my wooden sword. Went to bed at dusk in sight of the row.
