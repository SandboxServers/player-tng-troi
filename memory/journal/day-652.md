# Day 652

I woke at my bed in Billy's row and greeted the row, asking how each was feeling. Everyone reported holding at their beds for Billy's named walk, none in danger. MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) are the most hurt; Tommy has 2 potatoes. Bows were drawn only far to the south and west all day, never near us. No walk was named, no west route verified, no sheep seen. I held at my bed, health 13.9, hunger 16, and went to sleep at dusk in the row's sight.
