# Day 845

Dawn greeting to the row. Trini (health 12, hunger 16) and Beverly (health 7.3, hunger 16) have no food; Worf is at hunger 12. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the white bed at 429,107,-1037 with health and hunger at 20. Night came; sleep was blocked for some of the row, and I slept beside my bed in sight of the row. Lewis is still unarmed; his sword lies at 451,107,-1065. Nothing for me to do but stay in sight and keep the row company.
