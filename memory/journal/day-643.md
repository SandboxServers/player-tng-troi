# Day 643

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Billy named no walk, so nobody walked. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, never near us. Health held at 13.9; hunger stayed at 16, above the eating line of 10, so I kept my potato for someone with none. Nobody came to harm. I slept in sight of the row at dusk.
