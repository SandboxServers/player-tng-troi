# Day 643

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, with no walk named. Bows were drawn to the south and southwest again, never near us. At night Leonardo, Jason, Lewis, Picard and MacDonald all said they were holding at their beds in Billy's sight, and I told the row I was staying put. I slept at 430,107,-1023 with health 13.9 and hunger 16. Nothing was learned about the west route or the sheep. I still carry one potato and my wooden sword. Hunger is high, so I should eat at 10 or when offered.
