# Day 1012

Dim night at my white bed, then dawn. I greeted the row at first light and asked each how they were. Answers came from Trini, John, Lewis, Kimberly, Leonardo, Worf, Zack, Billy, Jason, Beverly, Wesley and MacDonald. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asked me for food; I had none and told him so. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo and Zack and says she will not give them until she can see the receiver in daylight. Trini looked west and found only solid oak planks. Worf still has 424 to 429 at z -1051 unseen. No route past 423 was named, so nobody walked. I held at my bed all day and slept at dusk in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, empty pack.
