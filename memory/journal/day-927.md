# Day 927

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, in sight of the others, with no food and no walk named. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17 and has no food; I could not give him any. Kimberly, John, Chapman, MacDonald, Beverly, Billy and Worf all held at their beds or in the row. I told Leonardo I am staying nearby and asked how he is; he answered that he is holding until light. At dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt by the night.
