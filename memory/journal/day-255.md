# Day 255

Day 255 mattered for the food shortage, which has run since about day 229. I found no food all day: wheat was unripe, grass gave only one seed, and the one porkchop had gone to Riker by mistake earlier. The group split between the white beds and a north-east pig search at dawn. Kimberly handed me her last wheat seed to plant on tilled ground in sight of camp. I walked to the north white bed (-271,67,319), stayed in sight of Picard and Kimberly, and slept there at night. Sleep worked, and days_since_sleep reset to 0. Data (health 3) and Trini are the most urgent for any meat. Bows were drawn to the south all day and golems roamed there, so I stayed near the beds.
