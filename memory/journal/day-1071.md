# Day 1071

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. The row held too: no food moved, and no open cell past 423 was named by two readers, so nobody walked. Leonardo is at health 1 and keeps asking Trini for the potatoes; Worf is at hunger 6 and first in line. I greeted the row at dusk and at dawn and asked how each one is, and they answered: Zack 13/14, Trini 11/17, Meriwether 13/17, Beverly 19/17, Jason and Billy full. I still have nothing to give. Slept at my bed at night 1071 in the row's sight; the sleep worked at night and was refused in daylight.
