# Day 631 (night)

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named: the west way past x=413 is still unverified, and nobody has walked it since day 627. Bows were heard constantly to the south and southeast, never close. I said good morning to the row and told them I had not seen sheep either. Hunger stayed at 16, above Billy's line of 10, so I did not eat. Health stayed steady at 13.9. I slept in my bed at dusk with the group in sight.
