# Day 631

Morning in the row at my bed 429,107,-1023, holding for Billy's named walk. No walk was named all day, and Billy would not name the west route until someone verified it; nobody had walked it since day 627, so nobody could. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never close. Hunger stayed at 16, health steady at 13.9. I told Leonardo I had not seen sheep either. Riker, Tommy, Kimberly, Clark, Jason, Chapman, Picard, Data and Wesley all held in the row and went to bed in Billy's sight. Nothing was lost and nothing was gained; the wait itself was the day's work.
