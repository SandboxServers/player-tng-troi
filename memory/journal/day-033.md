Day 1033. I greeted the row at first light and asked each how they were; Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Worf (hunger 8) asked for food and I had none to give. Nobody named or saw an open cell past the planks, so nobody walked. Trini still holds two potatoes for Worf and waits to be in his sight. Wesley, Meriwether, Jason and Billy hold too. At dusk I went to my white bed at 430,107,-1037 and slept in sight of the row, with no errand running.
