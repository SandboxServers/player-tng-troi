# Day 916
Held at my white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food to give. Greeted the row at first light and asked how each was holding; Billy, Kimberly, Jason, Beverly and Macdonald answered, Leonardo still weak and waiting on Trini's potatoes. Worf's walks (south-east, then north-east) were blocked or turned back, and the row is walled in with planks; I did not walk. Slept at dusk in the row's sight.
