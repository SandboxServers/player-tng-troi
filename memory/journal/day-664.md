# Day 664

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Greeted the row and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Data (health 13, hunger 17) both said steady and holding. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at my bed in the row and did not move. Bows were drawn to the south nearly all day, sometimes southeast and west. Sword handoffs went on between Billy, Clark, Wesley and John; I stayed out of it. My health stayed at 14 and hunger at 16, so the potato stays with me for whoever hits hunger 10 first. Went to bed at dusk in Billy's sight.
