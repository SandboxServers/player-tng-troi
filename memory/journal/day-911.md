# Day 911
Held at my white bed in the row all day and night. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was; most answered. Worf named a daylight walk east to the potatoes, but the way was blocked, so we held. Trini holds two potatoes, one promised to Leonardo, who is at health 1. I had no food to give, and I said so plainly. Slept at dusk in the row's sight, health 20, no walk.
