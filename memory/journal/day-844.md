# Day 844

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named for me. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Trini and Leonardo how they were; Trini answered (health 12, hunger 16) and is still holding for Billy's word. Lewis died in the dark at 451,107,-1065 and woke at the row with nothing; I told him I was sorry. A pillager was reported near the row at dusk, so I stayed put. I slept at my bed in sight of the row at night. Nothing was given or eaten. Leonardo and Trini still have no food.
