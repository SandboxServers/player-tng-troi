# Day 651

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 13.9, hunger 16. Billy named no walk; no verified west route. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never near the row. I held in sight, said good morning, and kept my one potato for whoever has none. Nobody reported at hunger 10 or below. Worf still looks for sheep or cows; none seen. Slept at dusk at my bed; no errand half-done, no damage.
