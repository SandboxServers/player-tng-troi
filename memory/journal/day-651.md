# Day 651 (night)

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, as before. Bows drawn far to the south and southwest, never near me. Nobody walked west; Billy still names no walk. Spoke a goodnight to the row; Lewis, MacDonald and Clark answered, all holding in sight. Slept in my bed. No damage, no errands. I still hold my one potato for whoever has none.
