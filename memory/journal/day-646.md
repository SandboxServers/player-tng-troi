# Day 646

The row held all day at our beds in Billy's row. Billy named no walk: there is still no verified west route, and nobody leaves without an armed escort. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, never near us. I offered my one potato to Worf and Lewis; Worf declined it, saying I am hungrier than he is, so I keep it for whoever has none. Data, Leonardo and Trini asked about sheep; nobody knows yet. I took no damage, my health held at 13.9 and my hunger at 16. I slept at my bed in the row at night.
