# Day 646

The row held all day at our beds in Billy's row. No walk was named, because Billy has no verified west route and will not send anyone out without an armed escort. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, never near us. MacDonald at health 7 now holds five potatoes, so my one potato is spare for Worf or Lewis when they ask. Wesley's way to his bed was blocked, and Data and Leonardo are still asking where sheep graze. I took no damage, my health held at 13.9 and my hunger at 16. I slept at my bed in the row.
