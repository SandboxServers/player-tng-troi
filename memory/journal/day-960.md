# Day 960

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, healthy and fed, with nothing in my pack. Greeted the row at dawn and listened. Trini and Beverly are still holding for Billy to name a daylight route; Worf is at hunger 10 with no food, and Leonardo has not answered. Billy is still checking for open air and no route has been named. I held at my bed all day, because walking without a named route is the danger I must avoid. I have no food to share. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of others.
