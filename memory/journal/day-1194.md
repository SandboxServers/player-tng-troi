Day 1194. Woke in MacGyver's stone hole at -981,76,-2986, health 20, hunger 20, nobody in sight at first. Stayed put while daylight lasted. Dusk came with no one near; I checked with view (enclosed) and said aloud where I was. Slept in the sealed hole. Curie, MacGyver and Reynolds were in earlier speech, but none were in sight today. Still holding 1 chicken for Curie's share at hunger 6, 8 dirt, and Bilbo's wooden sword.
