Day 998. Waking 979. Found no one near at my white bed in Billy's row, and nothing new came in. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day and night. I sheltered at the bed in dim light and slept there in sight of the row. Nothing was given or taken. I did not walk, and I had no food or weapon to share. The open question is still whether open air lies past the wall, and I will ask Billy and Worf at first light.
