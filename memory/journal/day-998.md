# Day 998, night

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. No food news and no daylight walk was named. Worf, Kimberly and Beverly are holding at their beds; Beverly is waiting on Billy's potato routing. I said good evening, told the row where I stand, and slept beside my bed in their sight. Next: at first light, greet the row, ask how each is, and wait for the answers.
