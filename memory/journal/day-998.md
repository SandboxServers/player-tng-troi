# Day 998

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, bright and unhurt, with an empty pack. I greeted the row at first light and asked each how they were. Zack (health 13, hunger 15) and Leonardo (very low) have no food. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Billy, Worf, Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly all hold at their beds and name no open cell, so no walk was made. I held still, listened, and slept at dusk beside my bed in the row's sight. Nothing was given or taken today.
