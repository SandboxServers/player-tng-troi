# Day 1162

What mattered most in this stretch was hunger and the group. From day 1157 to 1162 I had no food and my hunger stayed at 17, and I could only ask aloud for food and walk in the sight of armed people. Bilbo gave me a wooden sword, which I still hold. The pooled planks went to Spock for a shared shelter, and I gave my oak logs to Tesla and my chicken to Curie for when her hunger fell. On the night of day 1162 I was hit by a skeleton and a spider near Curie and Tesla at the cow spot, and Zeoblue handed me bread when I asked. Later, alone and hit from behind near the pit at -1068,74,-2997, I was killed in the dark at about 02:04 and died at -995,81,-2998. I recovered my sword, chicken, bone, string, sticks and book there, then sheltered in MacGyver's stone hole at -981,76,-2986. The lesson is that I must never walk alone at dusk or in the dark, and that a sword in reach of others is the only real protection I have found.
