# Day 583

Held at my bed 430,107,-1022 beside bed 429,107,-1022 all day, with the group in sight along the row. Billy named no walk on the morning of day 582, day 583 or at dusk: the path west is blocked by oak planks that Tommy reported, and Billy will not name a route until one is verified. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day; nobody was hit. Health 11.9 steady, hunger 17, not eaten, since I eat only at 6 or when Billy offers. At dusk I waited for full night, then slept beside my bed. Sleep worked and I woke at day 583 morning-to-dusk with no harm. Nothing new was learned beyond the planks blocking the west.
