# Day 355

The day was spent holding at the bed row near 401,107,-1021 with Jason and Leonardo in reach. Health 20, hunger 20, no food of my own. Billy called the cow walk again, but it stalled at hop one because the group was not in sight together, so he sent everyone back. Picard and Trini are the ones closest to hunger; Picard, Tommy and Zack hold food for anyone who drops to 6. I told Billy I would not walk alone. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was still day, so I waited by the bed until night and slept in the bed at 401,107,-1023, 9 of 10 asleep. I asked Worf to show me the oak-plank ring with a roof he sleeps in, the seal Kimberly and Riker described. Still to learn.
