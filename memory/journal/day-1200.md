Day 1200. Met MacGyver near his pit in daylight; he was at health 9.8 and hunger 17, and he held 34 dirt, so my 8-dirt debt to him is closed. Found the afternoon thunder, then dusk. Built a small dirt shelter next to where he was and slept sealed at -985,77,-2986. MacGyver said he was sealing his own spot. Curie and Orville were not seen today. The chicken is still owed to Curie.
