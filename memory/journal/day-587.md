# Day 587

Held at my bed in the row all day, Trini in reach, Billy in sight. Leonardo reported the west planks still block, and Billy named no walk. Billy offered two potatoes; I declined at dusk because walking is daylight only, then took no food when Billy said nobody is at 6 or below. Zack tried to pass me two potatoes but they stay with him as reserve. Health 13.9, hunger 16. Slept at my bed at dusk.
