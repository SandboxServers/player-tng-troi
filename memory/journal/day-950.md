# Day 950

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at first light. Beverly (health 19, hunger 17), Wesley, Billy, Worf, Kimberly, MacDonald all hold at their beds with no food and no named daylight route. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17, still asking for potato or bread; I told him I have none and that I am listening. Worf is walled in with planks and glass and no open cell. Nothing was walked, nothing was given. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of others; the night passed quietly.
