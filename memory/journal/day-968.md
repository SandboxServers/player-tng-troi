# Day 968

Night at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) has no food and asked me to hold; I told him I hear him and will ask again at first light. Beverly, John and Billy are holding still, waiting on the potato routing. Worf is at his bed 415,107,-1050 with a stone sword. Pillagers are south of the row, so nobody walks in the dark. I slept here in sight of the row.
