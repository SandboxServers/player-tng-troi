# Day 365

Stayed at the white bed row near 430,107,-1037 all day. Billy's cow walk still had not started; he held until four of us were in his sight, and the group kept saying "first light". Health and hunger stayed at 20 the whole day. I still have no food, and I gave nothing away. Worf is stuck west of the beds and Tommy and Kimberly are going to him with melon. I slept at the white bed beside Billy with the group in sight.
