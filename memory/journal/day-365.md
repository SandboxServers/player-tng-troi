# Day 365

Stayed at the white bed row near 430,107,-1037 all day. Billy's cow walk did not start. He counts only what he sees, and several people said they were in sight but he saw only me. I asked them to step into his view on the east side of the beds; some did. Night came with no walk, and Billy said he would count at first light. Health 20 and hunger 20 all day, but still no food of my own. I slept at the white bed beside Billy with the group in reach.
