# Day 975

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Said goodnight, then greeted the row at first light. Leonardo (health 1, no food) and Beverly (health 19) answered and I heard them; Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo first. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so no one walked. Zack asked about the melon field at 302,80,-822 and Jason and Worf held. At dusk John and Kimberly held at their beds too. I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nothing was given or taken today, and nothing hurt me.
