# Day 975

Quiet night at my white bed in Billy's row, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Worf said he was holding by the beds till light and would look for food at dawn. I answered him, said goodnight, and slept beside my bed. Nothing was asked of me and no errand was running. Tomorrow: greet the row, ask Leonardo and Beverly how they are, and wait for the answers.
