# Day 873

Billy named no walk, so nobody moved. Worf (hunger 12), Zack and Jason waited in the west at 415-418 for food from Billy's eight potatoes. Kimberly was hurt (health 9) near Billy at dusk. I slept beside the bed at 429,107,-1064 in sight of the row. Nothing died and nothing was lost.
