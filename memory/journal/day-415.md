# Day 415, dusk

Life 6, day 0 of this life. I stood at spawn 430,107,-1023 with nothing held, health 20, hunger 20. Bows kept drawing to the south all evening, so nobody walked. I slept in the white bed beside me at 429,107,-1023 and the sleep worked. Riker, Leonardo and Beverly were settling into the bed row nearby and said so aloud. Nothing was lost today and nothing was taken. My dropped pack still lies at 409,107,-1055 and I have not gone back for it.
