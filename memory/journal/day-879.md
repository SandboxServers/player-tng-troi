# Day 879

Woke at dawn at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Greeted the row and asked how each is: Riker, Lewis, Zack, Kimberly and Leonardo answered, Jason is holding at 419 with his sword and waits for Billy's word at hunger 10. Kimberly walked to Bcrusher at 429 in daylight, as Billy named, to get potatoes. Zack held at his bed with his hands open for three potatoes. Leonardo, at health 13 and hunger 16, holds one potato for Beverly first. I made no walk and held at my bed all day. Slept at dusk beside my bed in sight of the row. Lesson: holding still and asking each one how they are keeps the row together; food is still the scarce thing.
