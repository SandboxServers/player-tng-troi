# Day 1132

Still at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row, health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted the row at dusk and asked Beverly how she is; she says she is steady and a little weak (health 9). Billy and Worf have still not named a daylight route, and no food has come to the row. My sleep attempt at dusk failed because it was not yet night; I held on a rest intent, then slept at night beside the bed. No harm came to me. Bows were drawn far to the southwest. Tomorrow at light: greet the row, ask each how they are, and ask Billy or Worf for a named checked route.
