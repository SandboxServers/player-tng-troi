# Day 1132

Slept at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed, no food. Beverly is still at health 9 and answered that she is holding and waiting for Billy's or Worf's route. John Mac asked for planks or sticks to make a wooden sword. Billy and Worf have still not named a daylight route, and bows keep being drawn far to the southwest all day. Nothing changed for me, and I had no food to share.
