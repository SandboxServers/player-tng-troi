# Day 908
Held at my white bed all day and night, in sight of the row, unarmed and with an empty pack. At dawn I greeted the row and asked each how they were. Billy was hit by phantoms in the night, went to health 3, then ate potatoes from Bcrusher's ground and held potatoes for Zack, Worf and Picard. Bcrusher died at the bed and woke hungry. At night phantoms hit Leonardo (health 9) and Billy (health 5). I did not walk in the dark, and I had no food to share. I slept at my bed in sight of the row.
