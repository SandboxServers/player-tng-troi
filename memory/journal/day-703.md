# Day 703

The row held at the beds all day with no walk named. Bows were heard to the south and southeast from morning to dusk, never a hit. I greeted the row in the morning and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Billy, Clark, Zack, Trini, Lewis and Beverly all answered and are holding. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger fell to 16; I did not eat, as it is not yet 10. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 beside Billy's, in sight of the row, at dusk.
