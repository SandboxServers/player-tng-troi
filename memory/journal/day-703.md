# Day 703

Dusk in Billy's row. Held at my bed beside the white bed, in sight of the row. No named walk; bows heard to the south and southeast, no hits. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Health 13.9, hunger 16, holding one potato and a wooden sword. Kimberly keeps the row's potato until hunger 10. Slept in my bed.
