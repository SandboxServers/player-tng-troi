# Day 963

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, 430,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Nobody named a daylight walk, so I did not move. At dawn I greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Beverly and Worf how they were; I heard back from Macdonald, John, Trini (health 11, holding two potatoes for Leonardo), Lewis, Kimberly and Billy, who were all holding at their beds. At dusk I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed in sight of Lewis. The sleep was partial (3 of 10 needed asleep). Nothing new was found; the open-air question is still open.
