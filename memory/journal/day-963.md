# Day 963

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo, Worf and Beverly how they are; all answered. Leonardo is the most at risk (health 1, hunger 17) and keeps asking for a potato or bread, which I do not have. Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. No daylight walk has been named, since no open air has been seen from the row. I held at my bed all day, said what I could, and went to sleep at dusk in sight of others. Nothing was taken from me and nothing was given.
