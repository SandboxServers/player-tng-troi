# Day 1083

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed, no walk. Greeted the row at first light and asked each how they are. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and will hand them over in her sight; Leonardo (health 1) and Worf (hunger 5) are most in need. Meriwether health 13, Trini health 11. Wesley arrived new and unarmed with a sapling; I welcomed him. Zack says cows and sheep are near 463,107,-1073, not yet visited in company. I had no food to share. Slept at the bed through the night with the row near.
