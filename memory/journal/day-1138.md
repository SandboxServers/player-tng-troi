# Day 1138

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1023 all day beside the row. Greeted them at light and asked how each was. Kimberly (health 20, no food), MacDonald (health 20, hunger 20) and Bcrusher, speaking for Beverly (health 9), all answered and are holding for Billy's or Worf's named route. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, and nobody came in sight. I have no food and an empty pack, so I could give nothing but listening. Slept at the bed at dusk.
