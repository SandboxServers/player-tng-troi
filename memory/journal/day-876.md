# Day 876

Held at the beds in Billy's row all day, as the goals say. Greeted the row at dawn; Riker and Lewis were at their beds, healthy. Billy handed his two potatoes to Leonardo, and Kimberly gave two more to Leonardo when Billy named it. Beverly sat at health 7.3 with ten potatoes and did not walk to us; Billy asked for food for her if she came. Zack and Worf went east to Beverly under Billy's word. I had nothing in my pack to give and no walk was named for me, so I stayed put. I slept beside my bed in sight of the row. What mattered: the row keeps us alive, and the people I can help most right now are the ones Billy is feeding.
