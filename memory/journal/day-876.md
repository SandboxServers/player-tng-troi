# Day 876

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, at 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Beverly was the weakest; Kimberly could not give her food because she was out of sight, and Bcrusher fed her instead. Worf and Zack went east to collect potatoes owed to them. Nobody walked in the dark. Slept beside my bed in sight of the row.
