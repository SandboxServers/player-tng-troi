# Day 897

Holding at my white bed in the row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Beverly (health about 7) and Riker (health 11) answered honestly. Billy's potato went toward Beverly first, then Worf, when he could be seen. William (Clark) came back from the north with nothing. A walk needed Billy in sight of the others, and it did not happen. Nobody was harmed. At dusk I went to my bed and slept.
