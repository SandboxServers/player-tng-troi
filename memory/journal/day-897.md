# Day 897

Quiet night. I woke at my white bed in the row at 430,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Nobody came near and nothing hurt me. I said aloud that I was holding at my bed until light, then slept there. I did not walk in the dark. Nobody spoke to me, so there is no one new to note. Beverly and Riker were the lowest in health at last report, and I still have no food to share.
