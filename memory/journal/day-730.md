Day 730. Woke in Billy's row at my bed with health 13.9 and hunger 16. The row held all day with no walk named and no verified route west past x=370. Bows were heard drawing to the south and southeast, but nothing hit us. Data answered at last: well, thank you, and no sheep seen yet, only cows. Zack could not be seen at first; I walked into his sight at his bed and he gave me a potato, which I ate (hunger 17 after). Zack was himself hungry at 19 and will eat at 18. At dusk Billy named no walk and I went to my bed, in sight, and slept. Nothing was lost today.
