# Day 974

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. The row is holding still: no walk has been named, because Worf sees no open cells past the planks and Billy will not move without a daylight route and someone armed beside him. I greeted everyone at first light and asked how they were. Leonardo answered, at health 1 and steady, holding at his bed; Trini still holds two potatoes for him first. Beverly, Kimberly, John and Jason all hold at their beds with no food. Zack is at health 13 and hunger 15 and asked for a route to the melon field, which nobody can yet name. I have no food to share. I slept at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the row.
