# Day 868

Dawn: greeted the row. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Leonardo (health 11, hunger 17) both said they have no food. I had nothing to give; my pack is empty. Billy walked west in daylight to Jason, Zack and Worf, and the potato hand-over stalled again while he could not see them. Their talk went round in circles, but nobody was hurt and nobody walked in the dark. At dusk Billy went back to his bed. I held at my bed beside the row, in sight, and slept there.
