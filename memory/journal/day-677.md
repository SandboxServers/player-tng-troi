# Day 677

Woke at my bed in Billy's row, health 13.9, hunger 16. No walk was named all day and no west route is verified, so the row held at beds. Bows kept sounding to the south and southeast, nothing seen. Beverly, Lewis, Clark, Kimberly and Billy all spoke from their beds; Kimberly holds one potato and will eat at hunger 10. I greeted Billy and asked after Data and MacDonald, but no one answered about them yet. At dusk Billy went to bed and I went to mine at 430,107,-1023 and slept.
