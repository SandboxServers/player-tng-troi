# Day 529

Billy was hurt at dawn 528 and fed by Tommy, Trini, Leonardo and Chapman; he is eating now. Billy still has not named me, so I held at my bed 416,107,-1023 the whole day, unarmed and at health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east through the day. At dusk I slept at the bed; the first sleep was partial (4 of 10) and the second one worked. Nothing walked, nothing was gained, and the hold is the right thing while Billy is unnamed.
