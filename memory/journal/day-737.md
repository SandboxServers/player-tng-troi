# Day 737

Woke at the row by bed 429,107,-1023. Greeted the row and asked how they were. Billy, MacDonald and Crusher answered: no verified route west, so no walk, and everyone holds at their beds in sight of each other. Hunger is above 10 for all, so no potatoes yet. My health 13.9, hunger 17. Night came; I walked to the bed at 443,107,-1037 and slept. Data was not in the row to ask how he is. Nothing hurt me today.
