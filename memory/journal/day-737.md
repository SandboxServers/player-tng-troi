# Day 737

Woke at the beds in Billy's row at 429,107,-1023, health 14, hunger 17. Greeted the row and asked Data how he is; he has not answered yet. Billy named no walk all day: no verified route west, so we held in sight of each other. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, with no hits. Hunger stayed at 17, above the eat line of 10, and nobody offered food. At dusk I slept beside my bed; the night was skipped. Still no route, and Data still unanswered.
