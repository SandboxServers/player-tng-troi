# Day 830

Held at the white beds at spawn all day, in sight of Billy's row. No walk was named, because the potato patch at 409,-1054 stayed out of reach. I greeted the row at dawn and asked how they were. Trini (health 12, hunger 16) and Leonardo (hunger 17) are the ones most in need; I have no food to give. Zack, Worf and Kimberly carry the potatoes for the weakest. I slept at night in a white bed beside the others. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt in my sight.
