# Day 1014

The row held all day. Nobody named an open cell past 423, so nobody walked. Trini keeps two potatoes for Leonardo, who is at health 1 with no food; Zack and Worf wait behind him. I greeted the row at first light and asked each how they were, and listened. I have nothing to give, so I told Leonardo to keep still and said so plainly. I stayed at my white bed at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, and slept there beside the row.
