# Day 1014

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, empty-handed and unarmed, health 20 and hunger 20 the whole time. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Leonardo answered: health 1, hunger 17, still no food, and a bow was drawn to the east. I could give him nothing, so I told him to stay still and let Trini's two potatoes reach him first. Billy surveyed 423-428 and found no safe ground path past 423, so no walk was named and nobody moved. Trini, Zack, Jason and Worf held to the same rule. Beverly, Lewis, Kimberly, Chapman and MacDonald all held too. I slept at my bed at night in sight of the row. Nothing was gained today, and nothing was lost.
