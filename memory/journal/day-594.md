# Day 594

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day with Billy and the group in sight. No walk was named; the west route is still unverified. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 13.9, so I did not yet call Billy. Trini moved to bed 429,107,-1037 and Wesley and others tried their beds, one of which refused "way blocked". Went to bed at night in my own bed, not enclosed, with the row near.
