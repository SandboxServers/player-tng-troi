# Day 701

The row held at its beds all day with no walk named and no route verified west. Bows sounded to the south and southeast, no hits. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Beverly, Lewis, Clark, Zack, Trini, Kimberly and Billy all spoke. Nobody has seen sheep; cows are known at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. Hunger stayed at 16, so I kept my potato. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 at dusk, health 13.9.
