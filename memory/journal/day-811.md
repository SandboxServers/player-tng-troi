# Day 811

I held in Billy's row all day beside the white beds, with Kimberly in reach. No walk was named, and the bow was still heard far to the southwest. I told the row I was holding, health 14, hunger 17, and I will call Billy at hunger 10. My bed attempt at dusk failed because it was not yet night, and a second use of the bed was refused as a bed. Sleep worked at night 811 beside the bed in sight of the row, first partial then full. Data has still not answered how he is; he was not in sight. Worf is still missing, and Billy keeps his three potatoes for him.
