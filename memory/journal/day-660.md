# Day 660

The row held all day at its beds in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Billy proposed gathering at the middle chest 447,107,-1037 and naming an armed escort before any west walk; Clark, Picard and Kimberly said they would gather there. I greeted the row and asked MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) how they feel; both report steady. I hold one potato and wait for Billy's named daylight walk. I slept at my bed 430,107,-1023 at dusk, health about 14, hunger 16, no damage.
