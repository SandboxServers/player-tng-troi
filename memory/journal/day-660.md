# Day 660

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of the group. Billy named a short daylight walk west to x=380 and back, for Billy, Picard and Clark. I was not named, so I held. The walk never happened: Picard and Clark did not reach Billy's view, and at dusk Billy called it off until a named daylight walk. I greeted the row and asked MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) how they feel; MacDonald said he is holding, Data said he is steady at 13 with hunger 17. Bows were drawn only to the south and southeast, never near the row. I went to bed at dusk at my bed, health 13.9, hunger 16. Only 1 of 10 needed were asleep, so the row is not yet all down for the night.
