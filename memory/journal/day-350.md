# Day 350

Waking at the white bed row near 416,-1023, alone. Billy's cow walk was on but nobody could stay in sight, so it kept turning back. I moved to the bed row near 402,-1039 and held with Riker, Zack and the others. Nobody ate; I still have no food. At dusk I went to the white bed at 401,-1037 and slept. Health 20, hunger 20.
