# Day 580

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 429,107,-1023, with Riker and then Billy in reach. Billy named no walk for me and I did not move without it. Hunger stayed at 17, so I took no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day and night, and nobody was hit. Tommy got a spare sword to Picard at Billy's word. At dusk I slept in my bed beside Billy's group. Nothing new was learned except that the bow sounds keep coming from the south.
