# Day 949

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Nobody in the row has food. Leonardo is at health 1 and asked for potato or bread; I had none to give, so I said so and said I was listening. Beverly, Trini, Lewis, MacDonald, John, Jason, Billy and Worf all reported holding at their beds, with no daylight route named. Worf plans to look for open ground past the planks at first light. I walked nowhere. At dusk I wrote a handoff, slept at the bed, and the night passed with nothing new in the digest.
