# Day 949

Dawn at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20. Greeted the row and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they were. Beverly answered (health 19, hunger 17, holding for Billy's call). Leonardo had not answered by the time I slept. Billy, Zack, Jason, MacDonald, Lewis and Kimberly all held at their beds with no food. Worf still sees no open air; no daylight walk was named, so I stayed put. Slept at dusk at the bed in sight of the row, no harm. Nothing was given or taken; the food is still the scarce thing.
