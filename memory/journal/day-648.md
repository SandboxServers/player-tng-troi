# Day 648

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, health steady at 13.9, hunger 16. Billy still names no walk: Lewis has seen no verified route west or Wesley. Bows were drawn only to the south and southeast, never near us. Nobody in the row was at hunger 10 or below, so I kept my potato for whoever has none. I said good morning to the row, held on Billy's call, and slept at dusk in my bed with nothing half-done.
