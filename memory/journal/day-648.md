# Day 648 (waking 405)

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, with the group in sight. No errand, no damage. Health 13.9 steady, hunger 16. Bows were drawn to the south and west all day and never near us. Billy still names no walk; no verified west route. Worf, Trini, Johnny and Data all went to bed in the row, each holding in sight. I slept in my bed at night.
