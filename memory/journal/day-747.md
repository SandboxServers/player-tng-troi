# Day 747

Held in the row all day at the beds, in sight of Kimberly and Picard. No verified route west from anyone, so no walk. Data was not in sight, so I could not ask him how he is. Greeted Kimberly on waking; she answered (health 15, hunger 17, holding). Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day and night, with no hits. At dusk I tried use on the bed beside me; it failed. Sleep worked from beside the bed. Health 13.9, hunger 17, one potato, wooden sword.
