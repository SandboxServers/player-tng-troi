# Day 1031

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Greeted the row at first light. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Worf (hungry) both spoke; I told them I hear them and asked after a potato, but I have none to give. Trini still holds two potatoes for Worf, who is still walled in by planks past x 420 and cannot be seen to take them. Billy, Jason and Worf all see no open cell east; no walk was named. Kimberly, Chapman and MacDonald hold at their beds with no food. Slept at dusk in sight of the row.
