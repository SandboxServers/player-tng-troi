# Day 780

Held in Billy's row all day at the beds near 443,107,-1023. Health 13.9, hunger 17, one potato kept for Billy's call. No walk was named and no one was hurt. Bows were heard far to the southwest all day, with no hits. At dusk I slept beside the bed; the first attempt showed only one of ten asleep, and the second, at night, worked. Nobody new came into sight. Data is still not in sight, so I have not asked him how he is.
