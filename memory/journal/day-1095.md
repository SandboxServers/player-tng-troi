# Day 1095

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Heard Trini, Worf and Billy at dusk: potatoes held by Trini for Worf, then Leonardo and Zack; food at light; no walk tonight. I greeted the row at dusk and asked how they were; no one answered before I slept. Nothing new was learned about open routes past 423. Slept at my bed in sight of the row.
