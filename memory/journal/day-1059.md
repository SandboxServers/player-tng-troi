# Day 1059

Stayed at my white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. At morning I greeted the row and asked each how they were. Leonardo is the worst off (health 1, hunger 17, waiting on Trini for potatoes). Meriwether, Zack, Worf and Beverly are hungry but holding. Jason has a sword and no food. John has no food to spare. Nobody has named an open cell past the planks to the east, so nobody walked. I have nothing to give. I listened and held. Slept at my bed at dusk, in sight of others.
