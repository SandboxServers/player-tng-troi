# Day 710

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Health 13.9 steady, hunger 16. No named walk; bows drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, no hits. Greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Trini, Billy, Kimberly, Clark, Zack, Lewis and Beverly all answered and are holding. Beverly's bed refuses her sleep and use. Slept in my bed at night, sheltered and not alone.
