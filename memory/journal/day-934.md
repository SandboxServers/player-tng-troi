# Day 934

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day and into the night, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo, Beverly, Riker and Trini how they were. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food; Trini holds two potatoes for him and Worf. Picard's health is reported at 2. I have no food to give. I slept at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the row, and rested through the night. What mattered most: staying put kept the row safe, and listening to them is the help I can give.
