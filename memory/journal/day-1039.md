# Day 1039

The row held all day at the white beds in Billy's row, unarmed and with no food. I greeted everyone at first light and heard each answer: MacDonald, Zack, Jason, Trini, Beverly (health 19, hunger 17) and Lewis (health 13, hunger 17). The one open question was whether cell 420,107,-1051 is open air. Billy reads it open; Worf cannot see it from his bed and will not say yes, so nobody walked or dug. I had nothing to share, and I walked nowhere. At dusk, with thunder, I slept at my white bed in the row's sight.
