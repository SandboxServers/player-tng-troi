# Day 1121

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted the row at light and asked how each was. MacDonald reported health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and pack empty. Kimberly is holding still and waiting for Billy's call. Bows sounded far to the southwest only; nothing came close. I have no food or weapon to share. At night I slept at the same bed, not enclosed, with the row near.
