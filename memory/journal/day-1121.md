Day 1121. Stayed at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted the row at light and asked how each was. MacDonald asked for a weapon; I have no materials to give. Beverly and Kimberly are holding too, hunger 17-20, health 9-20. Bows drawn to the southwest all day, nothing came close. Billy and Worf have not named a route yet. Slept at night beside the bed; no harm came.
