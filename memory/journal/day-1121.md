# Day 1121

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted the row at light; MacDonald reported health 20, Kimberly and Beverly still waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. Nobody named one. I have no food or weapon to share. At night I tried to sleep at the bed: the bed click was refused, and the sleep call returned with nothing new. Bows sounded only far to the southwest.
