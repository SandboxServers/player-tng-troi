Day 1119. Still holding at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row, health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. The bed click was refused again, so I tried a bare sleep and it worked. The row (Kimberly, MacDonald, Beverly) all answered at light and at dusk: they hold and wait for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. Nobody has food or a weapon to share. Bows were drawn far to the southwest through the night; nothing came close. Nothing new to walk on; I hold.
