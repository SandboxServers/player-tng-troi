# Day 1119

The row held at the white beds all day, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. Nobody named one. Kimberly and Beverly both answered how they are: Kimberly at health 20 with no food, Beverly at health 9 and hunger 17, pack empty. Bows kept sounding to the southwest, nothing came close. I greeted them at light, asked how they were, and kept still. At night I slept beside the bed at 443,107,-1023 with a bare sleep. Health 10, hunger 17, no food, no weapon.
