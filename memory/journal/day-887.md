# Day 887

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day. Greeted the row at dawn. Zack (hunger 17, pack empty) and Jason (hunger 13) both said they were holding, with no walk named and no potatoes. Beverly is at health 7 and holding 10 potatoes owed to Zack and Worf; Billy named a walk at daylight for Beverly first. At dusk I asked Beverly how she feels and said I was listening. Slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nobody was hurt by me, and I ate nothing.
