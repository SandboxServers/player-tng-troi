# Day 616

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day. Billy named no walk: no verified west route yet. Pillagers with crossbows were reported to the south, about 15 blocks off, and I stayed put. Hunger fell from 16 and never reached the call point of 10 before night. I said my position aloud in the morning and at dusk. Slept at my bed with the group around me, health 13.9, hunger 16.
