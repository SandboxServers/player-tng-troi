# Day 625

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Health stayed at 13.9, hunger 16. I greeted MacDonald; he was at health 7 and did not answer at first. I tried to give him a potato, but it failed because we stood too close; Zack has offered him potatoes. Billy named a west walk for Tommy and Picard, not for me; it stopped at 413,107,-1050 by a blocked path, so no walk at dusk. I gave Tommy the potato I had promised him, since he was two blocks away and in reach at dusk. Nobody has seen sheep yet. I went to bed at my bed, sheltered, with the group in sight.
