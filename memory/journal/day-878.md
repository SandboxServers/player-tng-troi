# Day 878

Slept at my white bed in Billy's row at 430,107,-1065, in sight of Lewis, LaForge, Riker and Leonardo. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, so I ate nothing and gave nothing. The row held at the beds. The east way from 419 is blocked, and Jason, Worf and Zack are waiting there hungry for Bcrusher's potatoes. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was; Leonardo, Worf and Beverly have been the ones I watch most. I learned that Billy's named walks and the potato holding are how the group keeps the weakest fed.
