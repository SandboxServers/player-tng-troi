# Handoff, day 683 (night, going to bed)

- At my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, sheltered in the row. Health 13.9, hunger 16. Holding one potato and a wooden sword.
- No walk named. Bows heard to the south all day, nothing hit.
- Said good morning to the row; Billy and Lewis answered. Data and MacDonald still have not answered how they feel.
- Next waking: greet the row, check Data and MacDonald, eat at hunger 10 or when offered, wait for a named walk.
