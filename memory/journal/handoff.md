# Handoff, day 1224 midday

At the Stores chests, -920,71,-3040, in daylight, in sight of Macdonald, Zeoyellow, Lewis, Picard and Zeoblue. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Not enclosed, no bed, 19 days since sleep.

Chapman has eaten Leonardo's porkchop. Leonardo still holds 2 carrots for Macdonald (17), then Zeoyellow (18). I am not in line for them.

Next: hold with the group. Eat when hunger is 6 or below (Stores chests at y=71, x -921 to -927). Find shelter before dusk and do not sleep unsheltered. Give Curie her chicken at hunger 6 or below (none held). Return Bilbo's sword (probably lost at -1007,78,-2974). Keep looking for Curie, Bilbo, Orville and MacGyver. Do not walk alone.
