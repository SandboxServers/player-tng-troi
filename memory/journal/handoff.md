# Handoff, dusk day 1100

At my white bed 430,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Going to sleep here. No errand, not in danger.

Row: Leonardo health 1, hunger 17, no food. Zack hunger 13 and Worf hunger 5 still waiting for Trini's 2 potatoes; Trini was walking west to come into their sight. Meriwether health 13, hunger 17. Beverly health 19, hunger 17. Billy and Wesley hold in the row; Wesley sleeps at 415,107,-1051 on Billy's word. No open cell past 423 has been named, so nobody walks.

Next at first light: greet the row, ask each how they are, wait for answers. Hold. Share food only if I have some.
