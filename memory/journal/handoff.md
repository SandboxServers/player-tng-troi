# Handoff, night 1063

At my white bed 430,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Slept here in the row's sight. No errand, not in danger.
Row holding at beds. Nobody has food to share. Beverly, Meriwether, Zack are hungry; Leonardo worst.
Next at first light: greet the row, ask how each is, wait for answers. No walking without a named open cell and an armed person in sight.
