# Handoff, night 1066

At my white bed 430,107,-1037, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No errand, not in danger. Slept.
Told the row I hear them and pointed them to Trini's bed for potatoes. No food to give.
Next at first light: greet the row, ask each how they are, wait for answers. No walk without an armed person in sight.
