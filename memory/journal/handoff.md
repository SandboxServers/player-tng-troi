# Handoff, dusk 1228

At the Stores chests, -920,71,-3040, in sight of Macdonald, Zeoyellow, Lewis, Picard and Zeoblue. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Not enclosed, no bed, 23 days since sleep.

Night of 1227 passed in group sight without trouble. Chapman has one carrot for Macdonald (hunger 17). I take no food while others are hungrier.

Next: hold with the group and do not walk alone. Eat when hunger is 6 or below. Find shelter or a bed before the next night. Give Curie her chicken at hunger 6 or below (none held). Return Bilbo's sword (probably lost at -1007,78,-2974). Bed row is at -944,73,-3046; Tommy is holding there and waiting for a leg with coordinates from Trini.
