# Day 628

Morning in the row at 430,107,-1023 beside Riker. Billy named no walk: the way west is blocked at 413,107,-1050. Hold at beds, eat at hunger 10. I said good morning and held. Hunger 16, health 13.9 steady. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, but no arrow hit us. Dusk came; I moved toward the bed at 429,107,-1023 and slept in the row, in Billy's sight. MacDonald was not in reach, so the potato is still owed. Nothing new was learned beyond Billy's word about the west.
