# Day 1036

Woke at first light at my white bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row and asked Beverly how she was. The row is still holding: Leonardo at health 1 needs food, Lewis is at 13 health, and nobody has named an open cell past 423 or a daylight walk, so nobody walked. I had nothing to give. At dusk I slept beside my bed in the row's sight. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt today.
