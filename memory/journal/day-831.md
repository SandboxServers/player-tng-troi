# Day 831

Woke at the white beds at spawn with health and hunger at 20 and nothing in my pack. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Billy, Jason and Worf set out armed for the potato patch, but the path was blocked and they turned back at dusk, so no food came. Zack, who had not slept in six days, was sent to a bed near the row. I could not eat, since my hunger never fell to 10, and I stayed unarmed. I slept beside the white bed in sight of Billy's row. The day mattered for the clear way Billy keeps the group together and for the reminder that food is still the open need.
