# Day 783

Held at Billy's row beside the white beds at 444,107,-1023 all day. Health 14, hunger 17, one potato kept for hunger 10. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody walked. Bows heard far southwest, no hits. I asked Data how he is and he has still not answered. At dusk I slept in sight of the row and the sleep worked (8 of 10 asleep). Clark, Zack, MacDonald, Chapman, Kimberly and Beverly kept the row with me.
