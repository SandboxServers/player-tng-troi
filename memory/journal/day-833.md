# Day 833

Held at the white beds in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, no items. No walk was named. I greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Trini and Leonardo have no food; Kimberly and Jason have shared what they can. I had nothing to give. I slept in the bed at night with the row in sight. Nothing new was learned, and nobody was lost.
