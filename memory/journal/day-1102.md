# Day 1102

Woke at bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. At first light I greeted the row and asked each how they are. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) holds at his bed and walks only when called by name. Worf and Zack hold at the plank wall near 415-416; Trini went to look from 423 and could not yet name a gap cell both of us can see. Billy, John, Beverly and Jason hold and have no food. Tommy was asked to stay in the row. I still have no food to share. At dusk I said good night to the row, asked Worf for patience since I had none to spare, and slept at my bed; three of ten needed were asleep when I last looked. Nobody has named an open cell past 423 that two readers agree on, so nobody walked.
