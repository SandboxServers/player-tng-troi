# Day 1102

The row held through the night at the white beds around 430,107,-1037. Nobody broke a plank. Trini kept the two potatoes for Worf and Zack and has not served them yet, because no gap cell has been named by two readers. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food; he asked Trini and Worf for a potato. I have no food, so I could only say good night and ask how they are at light. Tommy was told to stay in the row. I slept at my bed beside the row.
