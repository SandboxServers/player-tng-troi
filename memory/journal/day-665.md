# Day 665

A quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so I held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in the group's sight the whole day. I greeted the row in the morning and asked MacDonald and Data how they felt. MacDonald said he was thin but steady at health 11 and hunger 16; Data said steady at health 13, hunger 17, and reported only cows at 339,104,-979, no sheep. Billy said nobody was at hunger 10 or below and promised to feed anyone who dropped. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day, but nothing reached us. I hold one potato and a wooden sword, and I went to bed with health 14 and hunger 16.
