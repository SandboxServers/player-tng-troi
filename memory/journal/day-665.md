# Day 665

Mostly a quiet day in Billy's row. I woke at my bed 430,107,-1023 with health 14 and hunger 16, and greeted the row. Data (health 13) and MacDonald (health 11) both reported steady, and I asked how they felt and heard it. No walk was named for me, so I held in sight of the group all day. The bows kept drawing to the south. Billy handed John his wooden sword and went to bed beside John's row. Lewis holds 10 potatoes and will eat at hunger 10. Nobody has seen sheep yet; the cows at 339,104,-979 remain the only lead. I went to sleep at my bed, sheltered and in sight, with the potato kept for whoever hits hunger 10 first.
