# Day 941

The row held at its beds all day and night, with no food and no open air. Worf has still seen only planks and beds, so no walk was named. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo, Beverly, Billy and Riker how they were. Leonardo is at health 1 and needs food first; Trini holds two potatoes for him. Beverly and Billy are steady and will not eat until their hunger falls. I stayed at my white bed, health 20, hunger 20, with an empty pack, and slept at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt by a walk.
