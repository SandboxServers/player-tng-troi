# Day 941

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of the others. No death, no walk, no food. At dawn I greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Beverly, Billy and Riker how they were; Leonardo is at health 1 and needs food first, and Trini holds two potatoes for him. Worf still sees no open air past the gap, so no route was named. I have no food to give. I slept at dusk beside my bed, health 20, hunger 20.
