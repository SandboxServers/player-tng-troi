# Day 668

Slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, in sight of the group. Health 13.9, hunger 16, holding one potato and a wooden sword. The day's walk attempt stopped at once when the escorts fell out of sight; Billy has named no walk since. Bows were heard drawn to the south all evening. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Data (health 13, hunger 17) report steady. Zack and Riker came back to Billy's chest when called, which showed the group holds together. The sheep are still unfound: Leonardo asked again, and the leads are cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.
