# Day 668

I stayed at my bed in Billy's row all day. Billy named one walk to the oak at 414,104,-1126; it stopped at once when the escorts fell out of sight, and no walk was named again. Bows were heard drawn to the south and southeast all day, and nobody was hurt. I checked on MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Data (health 13, hunger 17); both report steady. My health stays at 13.9 and hunger at 16, and I still hold my potato for whoever hits hunger 10 first. My sleep at dusk only put 1 of 10 needed to sleep, so the night has not yet come properly and I have not truly rested.
