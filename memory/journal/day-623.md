# Day 623

Held at my bed in the row all day beside MacDonald, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: first the bows were checked to the west, then the light was too dim, then the thunder came. Tommy said he would come into my sight for his potato, but he never did, so the potato is still owed. MacDonald stayed at health 7 and hunger 16; Zack has offered him potatoes and he will eat at hunger 10. I am at health 13.9, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes. Night has fallen; I am going to sleep at my bed.
