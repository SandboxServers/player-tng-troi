# Day 997

I woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. I greeted the row and asked each how they were. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Worf went out in daylight for food, came back still walled in by planks, and named no open cell. Nobody walked, and I held at my bed. I had nothing to give. I slept at dusk in sight of the row.
