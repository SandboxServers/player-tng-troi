# Day 1149

Quiet day at the white bed beside the row. I greeted everyone at light and asked how they were. MacDonald (health 20, hunger 20, unarmed) and Beverly (health 9, hunger 17, waiting for Billy or Worf's route) answered. Kimberly asked Trini to give potatoes to Beverly first; I have nothing to give. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day and nobody came close. Billy and Worf still have not named a daylight route. My health stayed at 10 and hunger at 17, with an empty pack. At dusk I slept at the white bed as usual.
