# Day 1144

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted the row at light and asked each how they are. Beverly is steady at 9 and not yet at the eating line of 6. Kimberly and MacDonald are at 20 with no food to spare; MacDonald still wants planks or sticks. Billy and Worf have named no route or bed. Bows were heard to the southwest all day. Nothing was tried that failed. Slept at the bed at night.
