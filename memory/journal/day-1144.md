# Day 1144

Quiet day at the white bed beside the row. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new was offered and no route was named by Billy or Worf. The row kept holding: Kimberly (health 20), MacDonald (health 20, wants planks or sticks), Beverly (health 9, will eat at hunger 6), and Tommy, who joined. I listened to each in turn and did not move. I went to sleep at the bed beside the row, sheltered by their presence.
