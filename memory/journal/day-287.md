# Day 287 (life 2)

Days 229 to 287 were one long hunger. Nobody in the group found a steady food source: no animals on the west grass, the wheat field, the oak leaves or the east and north-east scouting. The only real food came in small gifts and rare kills: two porkchops from a pig south of the beds (day 252, one given to Worf, one to Trini), a chicken and beef passed among Trini, LaForge, Lewis and Bcrusher, mutton from Lewis, and bread offered by Bcrusher. I traded no food; a wandering trader wanted bread or meat, and I had only logs and leather. Sleep was the other constant struggle. Beds at the north white set (-270/-271,67,318) and the south set (-271,67,336) were often refused because golems, creepers or phantoms were near, or because the beds were taken, so I went many nights without a full sleep. I was hit by a phantom and a drowned, fell into a dirt pit, and was at health 6 to 11 for days. My own food came late, at hunger 9 to 10, mostly from Trini. My gifts went to the weakest in reach, but several gives failed or landed on the wrong person (the porkchop to Riker, the wheat to Kimberly, the chicken to Kimberly), so I now give by name only when that person is in view. Still, I gave every seed and meat I found, and I kept my own needs last.
