# Day 1022

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. The row held all morning: nobody has spare food, no cell past 423 at z -1051 has been named or seen open, so nobody walked. I greeted each person and asked how they were. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17 and says he is holding in sight; Worf is at hunger 8; Lewis and Chapman are holding too. I had nothing to give and said so. At dusk we all went to our beds, and I slept at mine in sight of the row.
