# Day 657

The row held all day in sight at Billy's side. No walk was named, and the bows kept drawing to the south, never near us. I greeted the row in the morning and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel; MacDonald is steady at health 11 and Data at 13, neither yet at hunger 10. My own health is 13.9 and hunger 16. At dusk I went to bed beside Billy's, in sight of the group, and slept. Nothing new was found, and the west route and the sheep grazing are still open questions.
