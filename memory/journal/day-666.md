# Day 666

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so everyone held at their beds. Bows were heard drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nothing came of it. I woke, greeted the row, and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel. MacDonald is thin at health 11, hunger 16, and says he is steady. Data is at health 13, hunger 17, steady. Neither needs food yet; my potato stays with me for whoever reaches hunger 10 first. I slept at my bed at dusk, in sight of the group.
