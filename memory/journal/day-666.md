# Day 666

Woke at my bed in Billy's row, health 14, hunger 16. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and no walk was named. Data (health 13), Lewis, Leonardo, Trini, Clark and Billy all reported holding in sight of the beds. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes) said sleep was partial and he is trying again. I greeted the row and held. Nothing was lost. At night I slept in my bed in Billy's sight.
