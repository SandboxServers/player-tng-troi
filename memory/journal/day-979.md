# Day 979

Morning greeting in the row. Beverly and Johnny answered, holding at their beds with no food. Leonardo answered: health 1, hunger 17, waiting for Trini's two potatoes, which she will hand over once he is in sight. No walk was named by Billy or Worf; Worf is still walled in by planks. I stayed at my white bed 430,107,-1037, unarmed, empty-handed, and kept to the row. I wrote the handoff and slept at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing was given or walked. Still no food in my pack.
