# Day 782

Held in Billy's row all day beside the bed at 444,107,-1023. No walk named by Billy, so no walk taken. Health 13.9, hunger 17, one potato kept for hunger 10. Bows heard far southwest all day, no hits. Asked Data how he is; he has still not answered and was not in sight. Clark, Chapman, MacDonald, Zack and Beverly all spoke and held for Billy's call. Chapman gave the potato question to Billy first. Slept at dusk beside the bed in sight of others.
