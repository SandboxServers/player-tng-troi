# Day 952

I woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, with no food. Billy and Zack could not get into beds overnight. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they are. Leonardo answered: health 1, hunger 17, no food, asking for potato or bread. I told him I have none and pointed him to Trini, who holds two potatoes for him. Chapman offered to walk with Trini to Leonardo on a daylight route. No route was named all day. I held at my bed, in sight of others, and slept there at dusk.
