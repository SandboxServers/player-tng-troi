# Day 1063, night

Stayed at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all night, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody walked; no open cell was named past 423. Beverly and Meriwether are hungry (about 17) and Leonardo was worst at health 1. Billy is routing potatoes through Trini. I said aloud that I hold here and have no food, then slept. Nothing new came in overnight except the row's words, which told me the same thing: everyone is waiting for food and a named route.
