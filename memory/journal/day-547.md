# Day 547

Quiet day at the row. I woke at 416,107,-1037 at night with Tommy beside me, moved to the row at 415,107,-1041 in his sight and said so aloud. No walk was named by Billy, so I held. I slept at the bed near the row. Tommy, MacDonald, Worf and Clark all report holding at the row for Billy's word, unarmed or with a sword, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Nothing was lost. Still no food and no weapon; hunger is 20, so food is not yet due.
