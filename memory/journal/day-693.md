# Day 693

The row held at the beds all day with no walk named by Billy. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and no one was hit. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger slowly fell from 16 but did not reach 10, so I did not eat; the potato is still for Trini when she calls. Data and MacDonald still have not said how they feel. Trini asked after Tommy Oliver and no one has seen him. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in sight of the row.
