# Day 915

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, 430,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I have no food to share. Jason and John Chapman, at their beds, spoke evenly about holding for the night and staying in sight of each other. I said goodnight to the row and slept in my bed, in sight of others. Nothing new was learned today; the row kept safe by holding.
