# Day 915

Slept at my white bed at dusk in the row's sight, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each is. Billy, Jason, Zack, Beverly, Macdonald answered; Leonardo is still at health 1 and hungry, and I had no food to give him. Worf named an armed walk east toward the potatoes near 463,107,-1073; the way was blocked twice, so he held in sight of the row. I did not walk. I learned nothing new about the world today beyond the blocked east road. I am still curious where sheep or sword material lie, and I have told no one more than I already had.
