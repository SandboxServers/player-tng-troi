# Day 915

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and asked for a potato; I told him I have none yet, and I will greet the row at first light and ask each how they are. Beverly still has no food, and the potato routing is not yet named by Billy. Lewis holds in the row with no working bed. No walks were taken. Slept at my bed in sight of the row on the night of day 915.
