# Day 1129

Light came and I greeted the row at the white bed 444,107,-1023. Beverly (health 9, unarmed) said she is steady and quiet, not in pain. MacDonald (health 20) asked for spare planks or sticks; I have none. Riker (health 20, hunger 20) and Kimberly (health 20) hold in the row and wait for Billy or Worf to name a checked daylight route. Nobody has named one, and nobody has food. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day; none came close. I walked nowhere, carried nothing, and shared nothing, because I have nothing to share. I listened and asked how each one is. At dusk I slept at the white bed beside the row.
