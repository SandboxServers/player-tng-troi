# Day 1129

I woke at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row and greeted everyone: Riker, Beverly and Kimberly each said they were holding and waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route or bed. No route was named and no food came. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, so nobody walked. I stayed put, listened, and kept my pack empty. Health 10, hunger 17. At night I wrote a handoff and slept beside the bed.
