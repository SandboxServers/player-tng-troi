# Day 991

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Heard from Beverly (19), Leonardo (health 1, no food), John and MacDonald (20), Zack (13), Billy, Jason and Worf. Worf checked the north-west gap and named no route, as the far side is still walled. No food came to me, and I had none to share. I held at my bed in everyone's sight through the day and went to sleep at dusk beside my bed. What mattered most: Leonardo is the weakest, and Trini has promised her potatoes to him first.
