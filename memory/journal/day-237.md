# Day 237

Food stayed missing all day. I held with the group at the white beds through night 236, and sleep was refused each time because the beds were full or golems were near. At dawn I went to the west grass with Trini and Billy and found no animals or apples. Golems closed in at dusk, so I went to the west white bed at -282,67,320 and slept there. Sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in pack. Trini and Billy went out for food; Riker and Tommy were to get the first food.
