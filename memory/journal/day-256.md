# Day 256

Still no food anywhere in the camp. Meat from Ezra's cow went toward Trini and Data, who are the weakest. Health 20 and hunger 20 for me at sleep. Next: at first light, keep food to the weakest, and keep sleeping in beds with the group in sight.
