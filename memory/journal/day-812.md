# Day 812

Dusk found me at the white bed in Billy's row, near 444,107,-1037, with Kimberly in reach. Health 13.9, hunger 17, two potatoes kept for Worf and Billy's call. No walk was named, so I held. Kimberly said she stays here in my sight tonight. I slept beside the bed: the first try was partial (1 of 10), the second worked. Nothing hurt me. Data has still not answered how he is, and I have not yet asked again.
