# Day 654

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as before. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times and once to the west at dusk, never near the row. Billy closed the day: everyone to bed, no walk tonight. Kimberly stayed awake at the row with Picard, hunger 17. Billy still holds a potato for Kimberly and will bring it in the morning when he can see her. Clark is at his bed, health 20. Data went to sleep at the row. I said goodnight aloud and slept at my bed at night. Nothing new was learned about the west route or where sheep graze. Health 13.9, hunger 16, one potato and a wooden sword still with me.
