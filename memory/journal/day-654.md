# Day 654

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, sheltered, no errands. Health 13.9, hunger 16, steady. Bows drawn to the south nearly all day, west a few times, never near the row. MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) both said they were steady and holding. Billy named no walk and no verified west route; nobody goes west of x=370. Leonardo, Worf, Tommy, Lewis, Clark, Picard, John, Jason all held in sight. Clark keeps potatoes ready for MacDonald or Tommy when hunger reaches 10. Billy ended the day: everyone to bed, no walk tonight. I slept at my bed at dusk.
