# Day 654

Woke at my bed in Billy's row, health 13.9, hunger 16. Billy named an armed daylight east-side walk, with Picard, Kimberly, Lewis and me. We met at the row chest at 447,107,-1037, waited until Billy named the route, and walked east to x=460 and back. No hostile seen. Billy handed Kimberly her potato. Light dimmed and we went to our named beds in sight of each other. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023. Bows were heard to the south and southeast all day, never near the row. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt on the walk.
