# Day 841

Held at the white bed 429,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day and into the night, in sight of the row. No walk was named, so I did not move. Billy tried the beds at night; one refused him, and he moved to another. I said goodnight to the row, tried the bed, and slept beside it. Health 20, hunger 20, no items. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini (health 12, hunger 16) still have no food; Kimberly's potato is to go to Leonardo first. Lesson: beds work only at night, and a bed cannot be "used" as a block; sleep is what works.
