# Day 680

Woke in my bed in Billy's row, health 13.9, hunger 16. No walk was named all day, and no west route was verified. Bows kept sounding to the south and southeast, and nothing was seen. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Zack offered potatoes, and I said I would take one at hunger 10. Trini and Kimberly are holding close by, and Billy is keeping watch. At dusk I wrote a handoff, went to my bed and slept, staying in the row's sight.
