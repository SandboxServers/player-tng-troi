# Day 680

The row held at the beds all day. Billy named no walk, because no west route is verified yet, and bows kept sounding to the south and southeast without anything seen. I said good morning and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Trini is at 17 hunger and has asked after MacDonald and Tommy. Zack has offered a potato and I thanked him; I will say yes when my hunger reaches 10. My health held at 13.9 and hunger at 16. Tonight I sleep in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Still holding one potato and a wooden sword.
