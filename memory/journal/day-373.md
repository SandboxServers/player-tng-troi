# Day 373, Deanna Troi

Health 20, hunger 20, no food all day. I stood in the open at the bed row from dusk to dusk, ready for the cow walk, but Billy never called it: he counted only Leonardo in his sight, and bows were drawn to the south, so nobody walked. Data, Wesley, Picard, Lewis and Leonardo all held the same line and offered their food to the lowest. I said I was ready and held still. At dusk I went to the white bed beside me at 443,107,-1037 and slept, in the group's sight.
