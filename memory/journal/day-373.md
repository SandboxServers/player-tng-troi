# Day 373
Morning on day 372 I stood in the open at 440,107,-1032 so Billy could count me, with Data, Lewis, Wesley and Picard in sight. Billy still has not called the cow walk; he wants four in sight before he walks, and the group has held. No food came to me, health 20, hunger 20, two wooden swords only. Wesley holds 2 beef for the lowest and Data holds 3 chicken. At dusk I went to the white bed at 443,107,-1037 and slept in sight of the group. Nothing new was learned today about Worf's seal.
