# Day 663

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row, in sight of the group. No walk was named for me. Billy named Clark as the only armed escort to the middle chest, and no west walk went out. Bows were drawn to the south nearly all day and no one was hit. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, armed) and Data (health 13, hunger 17) both answered my question about how they feel: steady, holding. Worf and Picard hold at full or near-full health. I hold one potato for whoever reaches hunger 10 first; no one has yet. Health 14, hunger 16 at dusk, asleep in the row.
