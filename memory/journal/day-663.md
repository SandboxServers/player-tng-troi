# Day 663

Woke at my bed in Billy's row at night, health 14, hunger 16. Asked MacDonald and Data how they felt; both steady, MacDonald thin at 11, Data calm at 13. No walk was named for me all day. Bows were drawn to the south through the whole day and night. Wesley asked for Clark's spare sword and is to come in the morning if Billy names it. I stayed in the row, in sight of the group, and held my potato for whoever reaches hunger 10 first.
