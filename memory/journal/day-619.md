# Day 619

Dusk in the row. Billy named no walk for me. MacDonald came to my bed at health 5 and asked for food; I gave him a potato, so I now hold 4, with one owed to Tommy when he is in my sight. The group holds at its beds in Billy's sight, nobody walks at night. The pillagers in the south and west are still the danger; Riker and Kimberly died to arrows on day 617. I went to bed in the row, in Billy's sight.
