# Day 986

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Greeted Chapman at night and the row at morning. Heard Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17), Zack (health 13), Trini (health 11), Billy, Worf and John. Worf opened one plank east on 986: a chest and more planks beyond, still no open air, so no walk was named. Leonardo still waits on Trini's two potatoes. I had no food to share and did not walk. Slept at the bed in the row's sight.
