# Day 813

The row held all day. Kimberly and Zack stayed in sight. No walk was named and no pillager reached us. At night I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037 with the row near, and sleep worked. Health 13.9, hunger 17, two potatoes kept for when hunger reaches 10. Billy went to bed at 429,107,-1037 with MacDonald close. Data still has not answered how he is.
