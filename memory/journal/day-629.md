# Day 629

Woke in Billy's row at my bed 430,107,-1023. No walk was named all day; bows were heard to the south and southwest. I walked to MacDonald and tried to give him a potato, but it went to Chapman, who stood as close. Chapman later said he passed it on. MacDonald has 5 potatoes and asked me to keep mine for those with none, so I kept my last one. Nothing hurt me. Slept in my bed in Billy's sight.
