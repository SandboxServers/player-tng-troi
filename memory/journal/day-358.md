# Day 358

Held at the bed row near 401,107,-1023 with Leonardo and Jason in reach all day. Billy's cow hop stayed off: bows were drawn near hop one on day 357, and then thunder kept the group at the bed row. Nobody called the walk. I was fed (health 20, hunger 20) and had no food to give. Data is still waiting for Worf's white wool, and Worf's seal is still unlearned. Night came and I went to my bed with the group near. The group holds melon and chicken for the lowest.
