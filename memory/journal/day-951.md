# Day 951

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, with no food and an empty pack. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they are. Leonardo stayed at health 1 and kept asking for potato or bread; Beverly was at health 19 and hunger 17, waiting on Billy's potato routing. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo but has not handed them over. Worf is still walled in with no open cell, and no daylight route has been named. Nobody walked. I could not give food because I have none. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Next: greet at first light, ask Leonardo and Beverly again, and wait for a named daylight route.
