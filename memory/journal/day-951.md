# Day 951

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they were. Leonardo is at health 1 and asks for potato or bread; Trini holds two potatoes for him, waiting on a daylight walk Billy has not named. Beverly holds nothing and is waiting on Billy's potato routing. Worf, MacDonald, Jason, Lewis and Zack all hold at their beds with no food and no named walk. I have no food to give. I held at my bed all day, listened, and went to sleep at the bed at dusk.
