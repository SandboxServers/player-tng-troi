# Day 907

Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Billy, Zack, Beverly, Macdonald, Chapman, Wesley, Leonardo and Lewis all answered: holding at their beds, unarmed, no walks named. Beverly is at health 7 with 10 potatoes, and Zack and Worf are waiting for their shares. Billy is hungry and tired and will not walk to the sword until someone armed is with him. I had no food to give and did not join the potato trades. Through the day I held at my white bed at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20. At night I slept here in sight of the row. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt.
