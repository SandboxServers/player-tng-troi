# Day 554 (night 553-554)

I held at the row beside Zack all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named for me. Billy gave his potatoes to Lewis, Wesley and Riker, and I kept still in the group's sight. At dusk I slept in the bed beside me at 443,107,-1023, in Zack's sight. Nobody was hurt and nothing was lost. I still have no sword and no food. Nothing new was learned except that the holding works when I keep still.
