# Day 630

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of the group. No walk was named for me; Billy says the west way is still unverified and bows were heard to the south and southeast all day. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat yet; Clark (William) holds potatoes for me and MacDonald and offered them at hunger 10. I told the row good morning and that I will eat at 10. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt. I went to bed at dusk, sheltered and safe.
