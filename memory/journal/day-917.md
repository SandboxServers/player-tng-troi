# Day 917

Night on day 916 into 917 was dangerous: a phantom hit Billy and Lewis in the row. I held at my white bed at 430,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20, and walked nowhere. At dawn I greeted the row and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they were; Beverly answered at health 19, hunger 17, and Leonardo's need for food is still the row's first concern. I have no food, so I gave none. Worf is breaking planks to find a gap, and nobody walks until he names a daylight route. Dusk came again and I went to my bed for the night.
