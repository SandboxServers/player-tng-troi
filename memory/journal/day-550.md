# Day 550

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk for me. Potatoes from the water farm went to Lewis first, then Clark, Riker, Data and Jason. The group heard bows to the south and east, so we stayed at the row. I slept in the bed beside Billy at dusk; sleep ran and I woke at dawn with nothing changed.
