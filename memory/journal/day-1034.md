# Day 1034

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. At first light I greeted the row aloud and asked each how they were. Kimberly, Trini, Beverly, Chapman, MacDonald, Worf, Billy, Jason and Leonardo answered. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Worf is at hunger 8. Trini holds one potato each for Worf and Leonardo and will walk only when a cell is seen open in daylight. No one has named an open cell past 423, so nobody walks. I have nothing to share. I slept at my bed at night in the row's sight.
