# Day 1034, night

Stayed at my white bed at 430,107,-1037 all day into night. Health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Nothing was asked of me and no one needed food from me. Macdonald held the bed beside me and said he would sleep there too. I told the row I was resting beside them. I slept. Nothing new was found; no open cell past 423 was named.
