# Day 418

Woke at spawn 430,107,-1023 on the morning of day 417 and held there all day, unarmed, holding nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Billy did not call a walk, so nobody walked. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and into night. At dusk, phantoms came on Trini at the bed row near 413,-1035; Trini fell to 7 health and Kimberly and Billy held the sword at the row. I stayed at spawn, moved one block to my bed at 429,107,-1023 and slept. My pack is still lost at 409,107,-1055. Food: Wesley still holds 1 beef for the lowest; my hunger is still full, so I did not ask.
