# Day 894

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Riker was hurt and hungry (health 11, hunger 17) and slept beside me. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was; everyone answered. Billy walked by daylight with Leonardo to Worf, carrying the potato held for him. No night walks. I held at my bed all day, with no food to share. Slept at dusk at my white bed, in sight of the row.
