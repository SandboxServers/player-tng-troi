# Day 644

I held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Billy named no walk; he said the west past x=370 is off limits and we hold at our beds. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never near us. I said good morning and that I was holding, and I kept my potato and wooden sword. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 13.9, steady. Nobody in the row was at hunger 10 or below, so no one needed food. MacDonald is at health 7 and was told to eat when he can. I slept at my bed in sight of the row, with Billy, Picard, Lewis, Jason, Tommy and others around me.
