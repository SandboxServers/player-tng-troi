# Day 644

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, and the west past x=370 stays off limits. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, never near us. I said good morning, offered my one potato to Worf when he said he had none, and kept to the row. Health held at 13.9 and hunger fell from 16 with no change. Worf (hunger 19) and MacDonald (health 7) are the ones in need. Sheep grazing and the west way are still unknown. I slept at dusk in my bed.
