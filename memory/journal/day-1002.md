# Day 1002

Quiet day at my white bed, 430,107,-1037, in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. At first light I greeted the row and asked how each was. Answers came from Kimberly, Billy, Zack (hunger 15), Chapman, Worf, Trini (health 11, holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo), Jason (wooden sword in hand) and MacDonald. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo (health 1, very hungry) spoke at night. No route to the potatoes was named, and I had no food to share. I held still all day and went to sleep at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the row.
