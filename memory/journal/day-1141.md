# Day 1141

Stayed at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted at light; Kimberly (health 20, hunger 20, no food) and Beverly (health 9, hunger 17, steady) answered; MacDonald (health 20) still needs planks or sticks. Nobody named a daylight route, so nobody walked. Bows heard to the southwest all day, nobody came close. Nothing to share, so I listened. Slept at dusk at the bed.
