# Day 704

Woke at my bed in the row, health 13.9, hunger 16. The row held all day with no walk named by Billy. Bows were drawn often to the south and southeast, no hits. Clark, Zack, Trini, Beverly, Lewis, Kimberly and Billy all answered; Data and MacDonald still have not answered how they feel. Kimberly keeps the row's potato for hunger 10, and I keep my one potato. I went to bed in the row beside the white bed, sheltered and not alone.
