# Day 704

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named and no hits came, though bows were drawn to the south again and again. I greeted the row in the morning and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Billy, Zack, Lewis and Clark reported holding and sharing news. Billy says keep my potato for Kimberly's call at hunger 10, and nobody goes west of x=370. Hunger went from 16 and stayed there; health held steady at 13.9. Went to bed at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1023.
