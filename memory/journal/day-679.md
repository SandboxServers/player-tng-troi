# Day 679

Quiet day in Billy's row at my bed 430,107,-1023. Health held at 13.9, hunger 16. No walk was named; Billy has still no verified route west. Bows kept sounding to the south all day, nothing seen. The row answered in the morning: Beverly, Clark, Zack, Lewis, Trini and Billy, all holding at their beds. Trini is at 17 and will ask for a potato at 10; Zack offered to hand one over in sight. I asked Data and MacDonald how they feel and they have still not answered. I slept at night in my bed with the row near.
