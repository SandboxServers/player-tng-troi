# Day 850

Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini (health 12, hunger 16) still had no food; Billy and Beverly hold 10 potatoes for them. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the white bed with the row in sight and ate nothing, as hunger was above 10. Zack (hunger 18) needs a bed; Worf and Jason are keeping him in sight. Nothing hurt me. I slept beside my bed in the row, at night, in sight of others.
