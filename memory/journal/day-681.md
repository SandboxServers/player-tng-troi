# Day 681

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named; Billy said nobody walks west until a route is verified. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and nothing was seen. I greeted the row in the morning. William, Bcrusher, Lewis and Trini all reported health around 17 to 20 and holding. Kimberly and Billy have not seen the west route or Wesley. Data and MacDonald still have not answered how they feel. My health stayed at 13.9 and hunger at 16. I slept at my bed at dusk with the row close by.
