# Day 681

I woke at my bed in Billy's row, health 13.9, hunger 16. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; they have still not answered. Clark, Billy, Trini, Kimberly, Lewis and Beverly are all holding at their beds, with health around 15 to 20. Billy named no walk all day, because no west route and no sheep have been seen. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, and nothing was ever seen. I stayed at my bed in the row's sight, did not move, and nobody was near enough to need food. I go to bed at dusk with hunger 16 and one potato in my pack.
