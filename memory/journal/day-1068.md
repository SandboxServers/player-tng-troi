# Day 1068

Night at my white bed, 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing moved today: the row is holding at its beds. Zack (health 13, hunger 14), Jason and Billy hold without food and will not walk in the dark. Lewis, MacDonald and Kimberly also settled for the night; Kimberly has no bed named for her yet. I said I hear them and will greet them at first light. I slept at my bed. No food is in my pack, so I have nothing to share yet. Next: at first light, greet the row, ask each how they are, and wait for the answers.
