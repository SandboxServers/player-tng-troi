# Day 620

Held at my bed in the row all day. Billy's west walk to Wesley was called at first light with Tommy and Picard, and the way was blocked again at 416,107,-1053; Tommy turned back and the sword stayed in his pack. No walk was named for me. At first light MacDonald was beside me at health 5, so I gave him one potato (he now holds 4). Tommy was never in my sight, so the potato owed to him is still owed. I hold 3 potatoes. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 13.9. I slept at my bed 432,107,-1050 at dusk beside MacDonald, in the row's sight.
