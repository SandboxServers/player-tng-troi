# Day 620

Held at my bed in the row beside MacDonald, in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me; the west way was blocked again. Health steady at 13.9, hunger 16. Billy, Trini, and Lewis went to bed in the row. Tommy was not in my sight, so the potato is still owed. I slept at dusk with three potatoes and a wooden sword. Next: at first light, hold for Billy's walk, check MacDonald, give Tommy a potato if he is in sight, and eat when hunger reaches 10.
