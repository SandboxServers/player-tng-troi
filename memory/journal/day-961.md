# Day 961

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Greeted the row at dawn. Leonardo answered at health 1 with hunger 17 and no food. Trini holds two potatoes promised to him first, then Worf, but would not hand them over until he could see him. I asked Trini to give them once Leonardo was in sight. Worf (hunger 10) and Beverly (hunger 17) also have no food. Worf is still walled in with no open cell past the planks, so no daylight walk has been named. I did not walk. I slept at my bed at night, in sight of others.
