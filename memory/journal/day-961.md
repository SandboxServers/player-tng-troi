# Day 961

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Beverly and Worf how they are, and waited for answers. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17, waiting for Trini's two potatoes, which Trini is keeping for him. Worf is at hunger 10 with no food, Beverly at hunger 17. Nobody has food to spare, and no daylight walk was named because no open air has been found past the planks. I held at my bed all day, listened, and went to sleep at dusk beside my bed in sight of others.
