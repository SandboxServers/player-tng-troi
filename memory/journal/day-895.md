# Day 895
Morning at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. I greeted the row and asked how each was. Jason and Lewis answered; Jason at hunger 13, Lewis full. Bcrusher reported Beverly at health 7.3, and Billy walked his potato in daylight to Beverly and then to Worf, waiting for each to be in sight before handing it over. Riker at health 11 and hunger 17 spoke of a failed sleep with monsters near. I had no food to share, so I said so, held at my bed and did not walk. At dusk Billy, Leonardo and Zack went to their beds, and I slept at my white bed in sight of the row.
