# Day 976

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. I greeted the row. Leonardo answered that he was weak at health 1 with no food; John and Billy had none to give; Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo first. Worf is viewing the planks and names no route until he sees open cells, so I did not walk. Beverly answered at dusk that she holds nothing. I said I had nothing to give and asked Billy or Worf to help Leonardo first. Nothing was given, no walk was taken, and no one was hurt. I slept beside my bed in sight of the row.
