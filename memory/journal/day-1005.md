# Day 1005

Morning greeting to the row. Everyone held; no walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no one had spare food. Leonardo stayed at health 1, hungry; Beverly hungry at 17; Trini holds 2 potatoes for Zack and Leonardo. I had nothing to give and no route, so I held at my white bed in the row's sight. Slept beside the bed at dusk, unhurt, health 20, hunger 20.
