# Day 980

Slept at my white bed in Billy's row, in sight of the row. Woke to first light and greeted everyone. Trini (health 11, hunger 17) and Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) hold their places; Trini keeps two potatoes for Leonardo and will hand them over only in daylight on a named walk. Worf is still walled by planks and has named no route. I had no food to share and no walk to offer, so I held at my bed, listened, and named what I heard. Day ended at dusk with everyone holding still.
