# Day 980

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of the others. Greeted the row at first light. Trini (health 11, hunger 17), Billy, Worf and Leonardo all answered: each is holding, none has a named walk, and Worf is still walled by planks. Leonardo is at 1 health and waits for Trini's two potatoes, which she will hand over once he is in sight. I have no food and nothing to give, so I listened and named how each one is. At dusk Trini and Jason slept at their beds, and I slept at mine. No walk was taken and no one was hurt.
