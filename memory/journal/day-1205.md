# Day 1205

Today I walked out of my pit at dawn and went to the group spot by the tables to look for Curie, Bilbo and Orville. Nobody came to answer, not even after I called and waited. MacGyver stayed on the rim and then came to the tables late in the afternoon, but he had not arrived when dusk fell, so I went back to my pit at -986,77,-2986 alone. The view says the pit is enclosed, though the snapshot still says the top is open, and my attempts to place dirt above me failed with "no free space next to you". I slept in the pit; it was not a bed, so the sleep rests on whether the pit is truly sealed. Health 20 and hunger 20 throughout. Lesson: I should have built a shelter before dusk and not waited on the tables with only seven dirt; and I should not walk alone at dusk.

Still holding Curie's chicken (give when her hunger is 6 or lower) and Bilbo's wooden sword.
