# Day 613

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health steady at 13.9, hunger 16. Billy named no walk; Tommy, MacDonald, Lewis, Trini, Picard and Beverly all held in the row too, with no walk for anyone. Nobody was hurt. At night I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned except that the beds in the row are shared by many of us. Still 8 potatoes and a wooden sword. Call Billy at hunger 10.
