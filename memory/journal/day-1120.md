Day 1120. Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted the row at first light; Kimberly and MacDonald both answered: health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. Billy and Worf did not speak. My hold intent failed twice, so I stood still by choice. Bows sounded far to the southwest all day; nothing came close. Hunger 17, pack empty, nothing to share. Slept at the bed at night; the bare sleep worked.
