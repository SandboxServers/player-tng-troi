# Day 875

Held at the beds in Billy's row all day, in sight of the others. No walk was named for me, and I was not weak, so I took no food. At dawn I greeted the row and asked how each was. Billy fed Leonardo first, then Worf; Kimberly held her potatoes until hunger 10. Nobody was hurt badly, and no one died. I slept beside my bed at 430,107,-1065 in sight of the row. Still holding, with an empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
