# Day 1135

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted the row in the morning and asked how each was. Beverly reported health 9, steady, holding at her bed. John (MacDonald) reported health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, with no planks or sticks for a sword, and asked Billy or Worf for spare wood. Bcrusher (Kimberly) also reported health 9, steady, waiting for Billy's or Worf's route. No one named a daylight route yet. Bows were drawn far to the southwest through the day. I had no food to share. My intent to hold at the bed ran out of time without news. At night I slept at the bed and woke with the night skipped. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.
