# Day 694

I spent the day holding at my bed in Billy's row, in sight of the others. No walk was named, so nobody went west. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, but no one was hit. My health held at 13.9 and my hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat; Trini will call me when her hunger nears 10 and I will offer her my potato. Data and MacDonald still have not told me how they feel. Trini has still not seen Tommy Oliver. The row is calm, and everyone is healthy at 20 except Kimberly and Trini, who are a little lower.
