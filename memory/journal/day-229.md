# Day 229

Woke with nothing in my hands and no food. Wesley gave me 3 oak logs, which I turned into 4 oak planks; my first gather of logs failed with no reason given. Food was scarce all day. The group searched the west grass, and apples went to Kimberly, who was fed. I found no food myself and said so. I held the planks and 2 logs for anyone who needs them, LaForge first. At dusk I went back to the yellow bed at (-263, 67, 353) and slept there. The main lesson: food is the bottleneck, and sharing what I hold with the group works better than working alone.
