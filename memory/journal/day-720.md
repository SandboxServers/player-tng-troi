# Day 720

The row held at its beds all day and night. Billy named no walk; every person who answered (Billy, Kimberly, Picard, Lewis, Beverly, Clark, Zack) has seen no verified route past x=370, so no one went out. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, no hits. Zack offered a potato and I accepted; it has not yet been handed over. Data and MacDonald still have not answered how they feel. Hunger 16, health 13.9, steady. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in sight of the row.
