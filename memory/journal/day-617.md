# Day 617

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day, health 13.9, hunger 16, and did not move without a named walk. Pillagers shot people in the south and west: Riker and Kimberly died, Wesley was shot. Billy named one walk, for Riker east to Trini for a sword, and named none for me. I offered my potatoes to MacDonald (health 5, hunger 17) if he comes within my sight; he has not yet. Slept at my bed through the night.
