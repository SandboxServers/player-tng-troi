# Day 626

Woke at my bed in Billy's row at 430,107,-1051 with health 13.9 and hunger 16. No walk was named all day, so everyone held in the row. I greeted the row each morning and asked MacDonald how he fared; he stayed beside me at health 7 and plans to eat a potato at hunger 10. Clark offered his potatoes to Troi or MacDonald if named. At dusk the row went to bed in Billy's sight, and I slept at my bed with MacDonald beside me. Nothing hurt me today.
