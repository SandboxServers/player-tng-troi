Day 1152. I held at the white bed beside the row all day, health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Bed use failed ("that block is a bed"), so I slept in place; that worked. I greeted the row at light. Kimberly is well at 20 and holding, and still owes Beverly food. Beverly is at 9 and holding. MacDonald wants planks or a stick, and I have none. No route has been named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day. Nothing was given and nothing was walked.
