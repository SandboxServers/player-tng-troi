# Day 721

The row held at its beds all day. Billy named no walk, since no verified route past x=370 has been given. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, with no hits. Zack offered a potato to Worf rather than to me, and I kept my own potato. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 13.9. Data and MacDonald still have not answered how they feel; I asked again in the morning. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in sight of the group.
