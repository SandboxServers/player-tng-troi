# Day 985

Morning greeting in Billy's row. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17, bed 416) and Trini (health 11, holding two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack) have not yet met in sight. Zack health 13, hunger 15, no food. Worf has no food and will look for some in daylight. Billy, Jason, Beverly and John hold at their beds. No open air has been found; Worf names no walk. I had no food to share and no walk to name. I held at my white bed, 430,107,-1037, in the row's sight, and slept there at dusk. Nothing was lost, no one was hurt.
