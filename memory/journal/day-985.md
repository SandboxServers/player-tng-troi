# Day 985

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. The way west is still walled with planks and glass; Worf found no open air and named no walk. Trini (health 11) and Zack (health 13) answered my greetings and told me where they stand. Leonardo (health 1) is still waiting on Trini's two potatoes, which Trini cannot hand over until she can see him. I shared nothing because I had nothing. I asked each person how they were and listened. Slept at my bed in the row's sight.
