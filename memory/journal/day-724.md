# Day 724

Woke in my bed in Billy's row at 430,107,-1023, health 13.9, hunger 16. Greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Billy holds the row: no named walk, no verified route west, nobody yet at hunger 10. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, with no hits. Picard, Kimberly, Beverly, Zack and Billy all held at their beds. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat the potato. I slept in my bed at dusk with the row in sight, and the handoff is written.
