# Day 724

Woke in my bed in Billy's row at health 13.9, hunger 16. Greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither answered. Billy held the row all day: no named walk, hold at beds, call at hunger 10. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast constantly, with no hits. I held at my bed, in sight of the row, and went to sleep at dusk in my bed with the handoff written.
