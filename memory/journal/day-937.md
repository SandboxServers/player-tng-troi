# Day 937

Stayed at my white bed in Billy's row all day and through the night, at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, no food and no tools. I greeted the row at dawn and asked how each person was. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and Trini holds two potatoes for him. I told him plainly that I have no food, which I think he heard even though it did not help him. Riker and Trini are at health 11, Beverly at 19. No one walked, since Worf still sees only planks and no open air. I slept at night beside my bed, in sight of the row, and I did not go out in the dark. Nothing new was learned about the land; the only thing I can help with is listening and staying put.
