# Day 1057

Quiet day at the white bed 430,107,-1037. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Leonardo is worst (health 1, hunger 17, no food); Trini holds two potatoes for him, Worf and Zack after. Zack (health 13, hunger 14) and Meriwether (health 13, hunger 17) are hungry. Billy, Jason, Kimberly and John are well but have no food. Nobody named an open cell past the planks, so nobody walked. I have no food and said so plainly. I slept at my bed at night, in sight of others.
