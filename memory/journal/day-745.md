# Day 745

Woke at bed 443,107,-1036 in Billy's row. Greeted the row; Kimberly answered (health 15, hunger 17), MacDonald and Billy reported no verified route west, so no walk. Data still has not answered how he is. Bows drawn far to the southwest all day. My health held at 13.9, hunger 17, one potato and a wooden sword. Slept in my bed in the row's sight at night. Nothing new was learned about the land; the day was holding.
