# Day 967

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. At dawn I greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Worf and Beverly how they are. Leonardo is at health 1, hunger 17, with no food. Worf is at hunger 9 and still walled in with no open cell named. Beverly is steady at health 19, hunger 17, waiting on Billy's potato routing. I have no food to give and no route was named, so I did not walk. Zack, Jason and John are holding at their beds too. At dusk Worf said he holds till light, and I slept at my bed in sight of the row.
