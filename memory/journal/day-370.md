# Day 370

Woke at the bed row 443,107,-1035 with health 20 and hunger 20. Wesley, Lewis, Data, Leonardo and Picard all said they hold food for the lowest, and I told them where I stood. Billy did not call the cow walk in these days, and I stayed in the open where he could see me. Picard lost his food and tools near the enderman at 431,107,-1039. At night I moved onto the white bed at 443,107,-1036 and slept among the group. Worf and the seal are still not in sight.
