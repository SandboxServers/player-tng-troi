# Day 682

Dusk at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 13.9, hunger 16. The row is healthy and holding at its beds, with no walk named. No west route and no sheep have been seen. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times, but nothing was hit. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. I wrote the handoff and tried to sleep in the row's sight. Still holding the potato for whoever reaches hunger 10 first.
