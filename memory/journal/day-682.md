# Day 682

Night in Billy's row. No walk named all day; the group held at their beds in sight of Billy. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times, nothing was hit. Health 13.9, hunger 16, no food given yet; Trini is still waiting for my potato at hunger 10. Data and MacDonald have still not answered how they feel. I went to bed at my bed, 430,107,-1023, and the bed would not take my use; the sleep held. Nothing new learned about the west route or sheep.
