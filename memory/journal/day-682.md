# Day 682

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, so no one left the row. Greeted everyone at waking; Billy, Trini, Beverly, Clark and Lewis answered that they were healthy and holding. Bows were drawn to the south many times through the day and nothing was hit. Data and MacDonald still have not answered how they feel. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat; Trini still wants the potato at hunger 10. Health steady at 13.9. Went to bed at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1023.
