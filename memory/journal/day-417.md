# Day 417
Woke at spawn 430,107,-1023, holding nothing, health and hunger 20. Billy never called a walk, so I stayed beside my bed the whole day. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, and nobody went out. The group held at the bed row and kept saying they were counted and in sight. Wesley keeps one beef for the lowest, and Leonardo is the next to take it. At dusk Wesley, Leonardo, Tommy and Beverly went to sleep in the row. I slept in my bed beside spawn with nothing to hand and no walk made.
