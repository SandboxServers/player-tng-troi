# Day 334

Quiet day at the bed row. Health and hunger stayed full. I checked on Lewis at dawn and offered company to the drops at 408,107,-1050, but he did not come into sight, so I did not walk alone. Riker, Worf and Data went north for sheep. Lewis reported ripe wheat at 463,107,-1087 and good birch and jungle wood. Macdonald, Chapman and Data slept at the beds. I slept at the bed at 387,107,-1079, with 9 of 10 needed asleep. Still open: the drops, and whether Clark's wall closes the beds.
