# Day 958

Nothing new came up today. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they are. Trini, Beverly, Wesley, Billy, MacDonald and Kimberly answered, all holding at their beds. Leonardo did not answer; he was not in sight. Trini still holds two potatoes for him. No daylight route was named, so I stayed at my white bed in sight of others with no food. I slept at dusk at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20.
