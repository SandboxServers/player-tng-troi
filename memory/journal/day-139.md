Day 1139. I stayed at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row all day with an empty pack and no weapon. Bows were drawn to the southwest through the day and night, so I did not walk. I greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was: MacDonald needs planks or sticks, Kimberly is at 20 health, Beverly is at 9. Billy and Worf have still not named a daylight route.
