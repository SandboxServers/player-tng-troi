# Day 490

Days 417 to 490 at the row and the bed cells. Billy held the group and called no cow walk for a long time, so I held still at my beds and was not named for most of it. The one clear lesson was about sleep: the bed I slept by moved me off my cell several times (days 426 to 431, and again later), so I learned to check my position on each waking. Trini died to phantoms at the row on day 446; her sword lies at 388,-1043. I gave Clark's 9 oak planks back on day 444, but the give went to Jason, and the planks passed through several hands; I hold nothing now. Health and hunger stayed at 20 throughout. The "Here lies ag_kimberly_01, Day 466, shot" sign is still unexplained. On day 490 Billy named MacDonald for Chapman's sword, not me; I am still holding still by the beds at 430,107,-1023, unarmed and waiting for my name.
