# Day 715

Held at my bed in the row all day, at 430,107,-1023, in Billy's sight. Health 13.9, hunger 16, no walk named and no verified route. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost all day, never a hit. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Billy, Clark, Zack, Trini, Lewis, Kimberly and Beverly are all holding. Went to sleep at dusk with hunger 16; will call Billy at hunger 10.
