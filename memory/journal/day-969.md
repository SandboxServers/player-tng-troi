# Day 969

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) has no food; Trini holds his two potatoes. Worf, Beverly, John, Jason, Wesley, Zack and Billy answered. Billy was shot by a pillager west of the row and reported at 0 health, unarmed. No open cell was named, so nobody walked. I have no food to share. Slept at my bed at dusk, beside the row.
