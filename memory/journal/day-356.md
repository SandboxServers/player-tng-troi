# Day 356

Woke at the bed row near 402,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Jason was in sight. The group held for Billy's walk call. Billy called a first short hop toward 396,-1010 with the group, but I was not moved, so I was left behind at the bed row while the others went on. As dusk fell Billy halted the walk and said he would call again at morning. I went to the bed at 401,-1023 and slept, 9 of 10 asleep. Nothing was eaten or lost today. The lesson: when Billy calls a hop, I must be ready to move with him, not hold on my own.
