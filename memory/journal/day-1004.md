# Day 1004

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night. No food on anyone's side except Trini's two potatoes, which are still held for Zack and Leonardo. Greeted the row at first light and asked each how they are; Leonardo is hungry at health 1, Trini is at health 11, Beverly and Wesley hold nothing, Kimberly and John hold in the open. Worf and Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. Slept beside my bed in the row's sight.
