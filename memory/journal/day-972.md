# Day 972

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. The row held through the night and the morning with no walk named: Worf still sees only planks and people at the gap, so there is no open route to the potatoes or melons. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; Leonardo is still hungry and has not yet received them. I greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Worf and Beverly how they were; Leonardo, Beverly and John answered and are holding. I had no food to share. At dusk I wrote a handoff, said goodnight and slept at my bed in sight of the row.
