# Day 683

Dusk came with bows drawn to the southeast and west but nothing hit. I stayed at my bed in Billy's row, health steady at 13.9, hunger 16. Billy went to his bed and Kimberly holds a potato for her own hunger. I greeted Data and MacDonald and asked how they feel; they have still not answered. No walk was named and no safe route west is known. I wrote the handoff and went to sleep in my bed.
