# Day 683

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again; nothing hit us. Billy asked for a safe route west and nobody in the row has seen one; I said I only know the east route. Beverly, Lewis, Trini, Kimberly and Billy all answered the morning greeting. Data and MacDonald still have not told me how they feel. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger at 16, so I did not eat the potato yet. We slept at dusk in the row.
