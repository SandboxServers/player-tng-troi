# Day 948

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at dawn. Beverly answered (health 19, hunger 17, holding for Billy's route). Leonardo was silent at first, then answered: health 1, hunger 17, no food, holding at his bed and will not walk without an armed daylight escort. Trini (health 11) owes him two potatoes. Lewis, MacDonald, John and Worf all holding; Worf still sees no open cell past the planks and glass. No route named, no food found. I had no food to give and said so. I stayed at my bed through the day and slept at dusk in sight of others.
