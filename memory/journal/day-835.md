# Day 835

Held at the white beds in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, nothing in my pack. Billy named no walk. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini still have no food; I have none to give. I greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. At night the row spoke: Leonardo, Jason, Riker, Chapman, Wesley, Worf. Nobody was hurt. I slept beside my bed in sight of the row.
