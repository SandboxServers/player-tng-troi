# Day 743

The row held at the beds all day with no verified route west and no walk. Billy, Picard, Beverly, Kimberly and Trini all reported health 20 or near it, and I stayed in sight at my bed in 443,107,-1021 and 443,107,-1036. Kimberly answered how she was, and she is the one who gave me a steady word this morning. Data has still not answered how he is, after many asks. Bow sounds came from the southwest and west all day, so nobody moved. I held at hunger 17 and health 13.9, and I told Billy I will call him at hunger 10. I slept in bed at night, in sight of the row.
