# Day 1021

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at first light and asked how each is. Johnny, John, Beverly, Jason, Zack, Billy and Worf all answered and all are holding. Worf is at hunger 8 and asked for a potato; I have none. Leonardo is at health 1 and asked for potatoes; I told him plainly that I have no food and that I would go with Billy or Worf if they name a daylight route. No route was named all day. I held at my bed in sight of the row, and slept there at dusk.
