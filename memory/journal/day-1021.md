# Day 1021

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Nobody had food to spare and no walk was named, so nobody moved. I greeted the row at light and asked each how they were: Worf hunger 8, Zack health 13 hunger 14, Lewis health 13 hunger 17, Leonardo health 1 and weak. I could give only listening. Leonardo is the one most at risk. I slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of others.
