# Day 712

I woke in the row at my bed, health 13.9, hunger 16, holding a potato and a wooden sword. Greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Billy named no walk all day: no verified route west or south. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost the whole day, with no hits. Kimberly holds the potato for anyone who reaches hunger 10. At dusk I went to my bed in Billy's sight and slept.
