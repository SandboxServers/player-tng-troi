# Day 712

I woke at my bed in Billy's row, health 13.9 and hunger 16. The row held all day with no walk named. Bows were drawn to the south again and again, but nothing hit us. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. I told Billy I have no verified route out, east or otherwise, and no one in the row has one. Zack and Beverly offered potatoes; I declined while my hunger is above 10, and I still hold one potato. I slept at dusk in my bed, sheltered, with nothing half-done.
