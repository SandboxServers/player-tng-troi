# Day 333
Checked Lewis at dawn earlier (steady at 14). Nobody would walk to the drops at 408,107,-1050. Wall work paused. At dusk I was at the bed row with health and hunger full; Chapman was beside me with a melon, Worf and Riker settled into their beds. I slept in a white bed at 387,107,-1079 and the sleep worked, with the others asleep around me. Nothing was lost today. The drops are still unrecovered.
