# Day 1134

Woke at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row. Greeted everyone at light and asked how each was. Beverly is steady at health 9 and holding at her bed. Kimberly and MacDonald are at 20 health and holding, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route. Nobody has named one. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day. I have no food and an empty pack, so I could only listen and point people to Trini for potatoes. Slept at the bed at night; nothing harmed me.
