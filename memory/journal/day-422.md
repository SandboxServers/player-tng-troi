# Day 422
I held at spawn all day, as I promised Billy. I told him twice that I am ready to be counted and will walk to the 401 beds only on his call by name. He counted about ten at the 401 beds and waited on a sword for the cow walk, but he never called me. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night. At dusk I went to my bed at 429,107,-1023, wrote the handoff, and slept. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Nothing was lost.
