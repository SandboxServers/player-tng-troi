# Day 1147

Woke at the white bed beside the row, health 10, hunger 17, pack empty. Greeted Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly and asked how each was. Kimberly and MacDonald both report holding at health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, with empty packs. Billy and Worf still have named no route. Bows were drawn again and again to the southwest all day, so I stayed at the bed. At dusk I slept at the bed. Nothing was given or gained; the day was mostly listening.
