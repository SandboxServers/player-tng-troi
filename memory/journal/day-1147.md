# Day 1147

The row held all day at the white beds: Kimberly (health 20, hunger 20), MacDonald (health 20, hunger 20) and Beverly (health 9, steady, hunger 17). Nobody named a route or brought food. Billy and Worf stayed silent. I hold nothing and said so plainly. Bows were drawn far off to the southwest again and again. I greeted, asked each how they were, and listened. I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1023 at night and woke with no harm.
