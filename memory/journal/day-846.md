# Day 846

Dawn: I greeted the row and asked how each was. Trini (12) and Beverly (7.3) answered, and Leonardo (10) said he is holding. I have no food, so I could give nothing. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the white bed at 429,107,-1037 in sight of the row. Kimberly holds one potato for Leonardo, and Beverly holds 20 potatoes and will share at first light if Billy names the walk. Lewis still has no sword and stays at his bed. I did not go to the far spot. At dusk I slept beside my bed with the row in sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
