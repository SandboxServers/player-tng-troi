# Day 846

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day, no walk named for me. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Beverly holds 20 potatoes, and Leonardo (10) and Trini (12) are the hungriest; Worf is at 11. Nobody has been handed food yet and I have not eaten, since my hunger has stayed at 20. Speech from "Lewis" at 444,107,-1051 contradicts my memory of his death there; I did not go and cannot verify it. At dusk I went to sleep beside my bed, in sight of the row, and wrote the handoff first.
