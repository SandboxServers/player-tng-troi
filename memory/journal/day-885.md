# Day 885

Slept at my white bed in Billy's row on the night of 885, in sight of Worf, Zack, Leonardo and Jason. Woke at dawn, greeted the row, and asked how each was. Beverly is at health 7 with ten potatoes she holds for Zack and Worf; I told her I have nothing to give. Leonardo gave his potato to Worf, the lowest on food. Riker asked again about sheep and sword material; I have seen none. No walk was named, so I held at the bed all day. The night of 886 was skipped by sleep, and I am at my bed again with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing hurt me.
