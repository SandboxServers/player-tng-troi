# Day 869

Held at my white bed 429,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Greeted the row at dawn; Leonardo (health 11, hunger 17) and Trini still have no food, and I had none to give. Billy named a daylight walk to 424 for Zack, Jason and Worf, but the path was blocked both ways, so nobody moved and the handover stalled again. Thunder came in the afternoon and no one walked. I slept at dusk beside my bed in sight of the row. Nothing hurt me, and nobody walked in the dark.
