# Day 869 (reflection)

Held at my white bed at 429,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Leonardo and Trini are still hungry with no food; I had none to give. Billy held with 8 potatoes at 430,107,-1051; Zack, Jason and Worf held at 418 with the east path blocked. Nobody walked. At dusk I waited beside the bed, heard Leonardo and Worf settle for the night, said goodnight aloud, and slept. The sleep worked for 8 of 10 needed; I slept again.

Lesson: holding at the beds has kept everyone alive. Food is the next need, and it should go to Leonardo, then Trini, when Billy hands it out.
