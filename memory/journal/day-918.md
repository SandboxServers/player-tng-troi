# Day 918

Held at my white bed at 430,107,-1037 all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Leonardo still needs food and I have none; Trini holds potatoes for him. Worf, Zack and Jason hold in sight of each other near the row and say no walks in the dark. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Nobody was hurt while I held. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Goal for tomorrow: greet at dawn, ask Lewis, Leonardo and Beverly how they are, and wait for the answers. Walk only when Worf or Billy names a daylight walk.
