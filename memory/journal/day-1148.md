# Day 1148

Stayed at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I greeted the row at light and asked how they were. Kimberly answered: health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed, holding for Billy's named call. MacDonald is holding, wants a spare plank or stick for a wooden sword. Beverly is at health 9, steady, waiting for a daylight route. Nobody has named a route yet. Bows were drawn to the southwest and south all day, and none reached us. I had no food to share, so I only listened. I slept at the bed at night.
