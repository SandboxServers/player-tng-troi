# Day 1087

Dusk at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nobody came near, so I said a short greeting to the row, promising I hear them and that I have no food yet. I slept in the bed. Nothing new was reported in the night. Still unarmed, still no agreed open route past 423 at z -1051, and the cows and sheep near 463,107,-1073 remain unvisited. The row (Leonardo, Worf, Zack, Meriwether, Beverly) still needs food; I have none to give.
