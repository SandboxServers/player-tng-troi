# Day 1093

Quiet day at my white bed 430,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. Greeted the row in the morning. Leonardo is hungry with no food; I told him I have none to give yet and asked how he was. Beverly, Meriwether, Kimberly, Jason, Macdonald, Chapman and Billy all held at their beds. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Worf first, then Leonardo, then Zack. Nobody has confirmed an open route past the planks at 423, so nobody walked. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed in sight of the row.
