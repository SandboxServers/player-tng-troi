# Day 669

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so I stayed at my bed at 430,107,-1023 in the group's sight. Sleep failed in the daylight, then worked at dusk. I asked MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) how they feel; both answered honestly. Clark offered potatoes to Data, who will take them at hunger 10. Bows were drawn to the south all day. I still have not found where the sheep graze.
