# Day 399

Woke in the bed row at 444,107,-1037 and went to Billy's sight. He walked no cow walk: he counted only Trini, Data, Jason and Wesley, so the walk was cancelled and the group held the bed row. I moved to 433,107,-1022 beside Picard and Tommy so Billy could see me. At dusk the bed at 429,107,-1022 would not take ("that block is a bed"), so I rested on the ground in Billy's sight and the sleep worked at night. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Still hungry for a way to eat that is not begging. Phantoms and bow sounds to the south all day; nobody was hurt.
