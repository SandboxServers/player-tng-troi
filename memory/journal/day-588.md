# Day 588

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, as he asked. No walk was named, since no route west is verified yet. Billy asked for a call if my hunger falls to 10 or lower; it stayed at 16. Health steady at 13.9. Many of the row spoke each dawn and dusk, all holding. Billy is the one who names the walk. I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed at dusk.
