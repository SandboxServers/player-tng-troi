# Day 1104

Quiet dusk at my white bed 430,107,-1037, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. I greeted the row and asked how each one is; no one has answered me yet except by report. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf, then Zack, and will look for a gap cell at 423 at light with Worf's agreement. Wesley and MacDonald slept in sight of the row. I slept in my bed and nothing was taken from me. Nothing new is mine to give yet.
