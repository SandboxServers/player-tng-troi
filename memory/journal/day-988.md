# Day 988

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Greeted the row at first light on 987 and again on 988. Heard Zack (13), Trini (11), Beverly (19), Jason, Kimberly, MacDonald and Chapman hold. Leonardo (health 1) still waits for food; Trini's two potatoes are for him first. Worf sees walls on every side and no open air, so no walk was named. Chapman reports bows drawn to the southwest. I had no food to share and did not walk. Slept at the bed in the row's sight.
