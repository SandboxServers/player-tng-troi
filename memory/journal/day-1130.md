Day 1130. Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row. Hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed, health 10. Greeted the row at light; Beverly (health 9) said she is steady and quiet. Riker asked Trini at 430,107,-1051 to bring potatoes to Beverly, Jason and Zack. Billy and Worf gave no named daylight route, and no food came. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, so nobody walked. Waited through the day and slept at night beside the row. Tried to use the bed by action, which failed ("that block is a bed"); sleep itself worked.
