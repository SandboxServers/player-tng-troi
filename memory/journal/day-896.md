Day 896. Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at dawn and heard from most of them. Beverly is lowest (health 7) and first in line for Billy's potato; I had no food to give. A pillager with a crossbow was north of the row at dusk, and Billy named a hold with the beds between us and it. I held at my bed and did not walk. Slept at my bed at dusk, with no errand running.
