# Day 910

Held at my white bed in the row through the night and into the morning, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Worf is at hunger 12 and waits for the two potatoes Trini holds; I had nothing to give. Billy named no walk, so I stayed put. Worf went toward the cows in daylight with Zack, Jason declined, and I did not follow. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing hurt me today.
