# Day 910

Night in the row. I held at my white bed at 430,107,-1037 with health 20, hunger 20 and no food. Lewis, Billy, Leonardo and Macdonald all spoke of holding at beds and not walking in the dark. Lewis's first bed refused him and he moved to the next one. I greeted the row, then slept in sight of them. Nothing was learned new today, and no one is hurt that I know of. I still have no food to give.
