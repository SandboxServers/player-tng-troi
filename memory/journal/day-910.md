# Day 910

Greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Trini, Jason, Zack, Lewis and Leonardo answered; Billy, at health 1, asked to stay in sight of Zack and Worf. I had no food to give and held at my white bed the whole day. Worf named a daylight walk to the potatoes, but it was blocked and nobody went far. At dusk I went to sleep in the row's sight, at 430,107,-1037, health 20.
