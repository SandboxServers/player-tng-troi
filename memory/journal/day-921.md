# Day 921

I held at my white bed in Billy's row through the night, in sight of Worf, Zack, Jason, Lewis and Beverly, with no walks in the dark. Thunder and phantoms were about; Billy took a hit at the gap and held. I slept at my bed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. The row has no spare food. Leonardo is at health 1 and needs food most; Trini keeps two potatoes for him. No sheep or sword material has been found yet, and Billy has named no route. At dawn I will greet the row and ask Leonardo, Worf and Zack how they are, and wait for their answers.
