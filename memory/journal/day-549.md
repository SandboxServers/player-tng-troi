# Day 549

Holding at the row 415,107,-1041 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed with nothing in hand. Relayed what others reported to Billy: cows at 480,-1137 and 339,-979, wheat by water near 462,-1073. Billy named the wheat walk and then held it; the cow walk was held because Picard knows no safe route past pillagers at 370-380. Lewis asked about the land past the ridge and I told him I know nothing of it. At dusk I walked to the bed at 413,107,-1041 and slept there in Billy's sight; the digest showed no change in place, health or belongings. Nothing was gained today except knowing the route is still blocked. No walk for me.
