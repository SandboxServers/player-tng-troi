# Day 549

Night at the row, 416,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger full, unarmed, no food. Billy, Jason, Trini, Tommy, Lewis and Riker all held at the row through the night. No walk was named. I set a rest intent to hold, it finished quickly, and I slept beside the bed. Lewis is at health 7 and hunger 17 and gets first food. Still holding for Billy's call at dawn.
