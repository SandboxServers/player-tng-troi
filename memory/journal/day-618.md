# Day 618

Woke at my bed in the row, health 14, hunger 16. Held there in Billy's sight all day. No walk was named for me; Billy named none, so I did not walk. Offered potatoes to MacDonald (health 5), who is waiting for Billy's daylight walk to Clark's potatoes. Dusk came with no events, and I am at my bed for the night, with a handoff written.
