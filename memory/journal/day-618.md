# Day 618

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 all day in Billy's sight. Billy named a walk only for Riker (to Trini for a spare sword), which Riker finished and returned from. No walk for me, and I did not call Billy since hunger stayed at 16. MacDonald (health 5) is still at bed 415,107,-1037 and my potatoes wait for him in sight. Tommy asked whether the west route was verified; Billy said no one has walked it. Slept at dusk in the row with the group, 8 of 10 needed asleep.
