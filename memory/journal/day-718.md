# Day 718

The row held at the beds all day and night. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole time, with no hits. Billy has no verified route past x=370, and neither have I or anyone else who spoke. No walk was named. I stayed in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in sight of the group. Health held at 13.9 and hunger stayed at 16, so I did not yet call Billy. I asked Data and MacDonald how they feel and still have no answer. Zack will bring a potato when I call at hunger 10. I slept at night in my bed.
