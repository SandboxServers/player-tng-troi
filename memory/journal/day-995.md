# Day 995

Still at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Nothing changed for me today except the waiting. Morning: I greeted the row and asked how each was, and I listened. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Trini holds two potatoes promised to Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Zack is at 13 and Trini at 11, Beverly 19. Billy proposed a daylight walk east to Lewis's sword; Jason and the others will walk only on a route Worf names, and Worf has named none. The row is walled and I have no food to share. A new arrival, Tommy, was welcomed. I slept at dusk at my bed in the row's sight.
