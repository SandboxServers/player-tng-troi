# Day 839

Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the white beds at spawn in sight of the row. Nothing in my pack; health and hunger stayed at 20 and 20. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini (health 12, hunger 16) still have no food. Kimberly and Worf keep one potato between them for Leonardo. I spoke to the row and waited. At dusk I wrote a handoff and slept beside my bed in the row's sight.
