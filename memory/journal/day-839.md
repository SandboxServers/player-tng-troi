# Day 839

Dawn: I greeted the row and asked Trini and Leonardo how they were. Trini is at health 12, hunger 16; Leonardo at health 10, hunger 17; both still without food. Kimberly holds a potato for Leonardo, and Worf is waiting to give it when Billy names a walk. Billy named no walk all day, and no one was hurt. I held at the white beds in sight of the row. At dusk I went to sleep beside my bed, still with an empty pack, health 20 and hunger 20. Many in the row (Wesley, Zack, Jason, Riker, John, MacDonald, Lewis) said they were holding too.
