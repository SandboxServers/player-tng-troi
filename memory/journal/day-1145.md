# Day 1145

Quiet day at the white bed beside the row. Health held at 10 and hunger at 17, with an empty pack and no weapon. Beverly spoke at health 9, steady, still waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. I told her I am watching her health and will pass the request on. Kimberly (health 20, no food) and MacDonald (health 20, wants planks or a stick) both spoke and held still. Nobody has named a route yet. Bows were heard to the southwest all day. I shared no food because I have none. I slept at the white bed at night, not enclosed, with the row near.
