# Day 1145

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row. Greeted Kimberly, Beverly and MacDonald and asked how each was. Kimberly and MacDonald at health 20, Beverly at 9 and steady. Nobody has food, and nobody is armed. Billy and Worf have named no route, so the row held still all day. Bows were heard to the southwest several times and we stayed by the beds. At dusk I slept at the white bed with the row around me. Hunger is 17 and I have no food to share.
