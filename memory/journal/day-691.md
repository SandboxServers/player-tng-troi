# Day 691

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day with no walk named. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, and to the west once at dusk, with no hits. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger at 16, so I kept my potato for Trini at hunger 10. Trini, Zack, Kimberly, Beverly, Clark and Billy all held in sight. I slept at my bed in the row, in sight of Billy.
