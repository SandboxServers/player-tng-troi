Day 691. Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 430,107,-1023. No walk was named, so no one went out. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times through the day, always heard and never hitting anyone. I told the row I am steady and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Trini is waiting to be fed at hunger 10, and I still hold one potato for her. I slept at my bed in sight of the row at dusk, health 13.9, hunger 16.
