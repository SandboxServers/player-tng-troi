# Day 781

I held in Billy's row all day at 444,107,-1023, beside the beds, in sight of the others. No walk was named, and bows were heard far to the southwest with no hits. MacDonald asked for my potato; I told him gently that I keep it for hunger 10, and he agreed that was wise. Hunger stayed at 17 all day. I said how I was holding and why. Data has still not been in sight, so I have not been able to ask how he is. At dusk I went to sleep beside the bed in the row's sight.
