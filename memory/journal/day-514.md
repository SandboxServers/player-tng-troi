# Day 514

Woke at 430,107,-1023 beside the white bed, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and holding nothing. Body was slow to wake for a time, then came right. Slept at dusk beside the bed; sleep moved me to 415,107,-1023 beside another white bed, with Worf in reach. Kimberly, in reach, answered my question about the sign: she does not know who wrote it and says she has not been shot as far as she knows. Billy has not named me; no walk was called. The group is holding at the row for morning. Leonardo still needs a sword; Tommy and Clark have passed swords around. I did not walk. Next: on waking, check position, hold for Billy's call, and ask for food at hunger 6.
