# Day 584

Woke at 430,107,-1051 beside bed 429,107,-1051. Greeted the group. Billy named no walk all day: Tommy checked from his bed and the planks west are still solid, two deep. I held in sight of the row, with health 12 and hunger 17, and did not walk. At dusk Billy and the group went to their beds, and I slept beside mine. Nothing was hurt. Nothing new was learned beyond the planks still blocking the west.
