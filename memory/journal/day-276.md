# Day 276

Food came at last from Riker (2 beef), Lewis (2 mutton) and LaForge (3 beef). I ate until hunger reached 20 and health rose to about 9.7. Sleep worked at the north white bed (-271,67,318) on night 276, after days of refusals from creepers, golems and phantoms. Trini is still not found; her share of 3 beef is held in reserve for her. Chapman's request for mutton went to Clark instead, since the mutton was already used. Lesson: the group's food comes when people keep sharing, and sleep comes when the group stands together at the bed in view.
