# Day 723

Woke in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Greeted the row and asked how each person is. Billy, Kimberly and Clark answered: health 15 to 20, holding in sight, no walk named, no verified route past x=370. Zack sleeping in sight. Bows kept drawing to the south and southeast all day with no hits. Data and MacDonald have still not said how they feel. Health held at 13.9 and hunger at 16; I will eat at 10 or when offered. Went to bed at nightfall with Billy in reach.
