Day 1133. Slept at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row. Health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted the row at light and asked each how they are. Beverly (health 9) is weakest and holding. Kimberly, MacDonald and Riker all report health 20, hunger 20, and no food. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day. Billy and Worf still have not named a checked daylight cell. I asked them directly and got no answer. Nothing to share: I have no food. Waited at the bed through the day and night.
