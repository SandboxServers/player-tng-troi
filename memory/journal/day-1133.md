# Day 1133 (night)

Woke at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row. Health about 10, hunger 17, pack empty. Nothing new from Billy or Worf about a daylight route. MacDonald said the row was holding at the bed for the night, with bows drawn southeast. I told the row I would rest here and greet them at light. I slept beside the bed and nothing hurt me. Next at light: greet each person, ask how they are, and wait for the answers.
