Day 1133. Woke at the white bed with the row. Greeted them; MacDonald and Kimberly holding at health 20, hunger 20 but with no food. Beverly is still weakest at health 9; I told her I am watching. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day. Billy and Worf have still not named a daylight route. I have no food or planks to share. Went to sleep at dusk at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row.
