# Day 640

The row held at its beds all day. Bows were drawn again and again to the south and west, and nobody named a walk. Billy kept the call: no walk until we had seen the danger ourselves. I told Chapman I was holding, and I stayed at my bed at 430,107,-1023 with health steady at 13.9. Hunger sat at 16 and I kept my potato for someone with none. At dusk Billy said to sleep at our beds and stay in sight, and I slept there. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt today that I heard of.
