# Day 640

Woke at my bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row with Chapman beside me. The whole row held at their beds all day with no walk named; Billy's morning call never came as a walk, and at dusk he said no walks until morning. Bows were heard to the south and southeast all day and night, and Kimberly was hit from behind near 413,-1041. I stayed put, health 13.9, hunger 16. MacDonald remains the weakest at health 7. The sheep question came up again from Leonardo and Data; nobody has seen sheep. I greeted Chapman and asked how they felt, and I took the row's reports as they came. Nothing I did changed the day; holding was the right choice.
