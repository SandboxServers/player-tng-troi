# Day 824

Hunger 17, health 13.9, no food, wooden sword only. Held in Billy's row at the white beds near 430,107,-1037 all day, as the goals said. No walk was named. Pillagers were last heard west and south-west. Trini, John, Leonardo, Jason, Wesley, Zack and Kimberly all stayed near, and most of them are also without food. I said I would sleep in sight of the row, and I did. Data still has not answered how he is; I have not been able to ask him yet.
