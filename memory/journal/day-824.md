# Day 824

Held in Billy's row all day at the white beds near 430,107,-1037. No walk was named by Billy, and no threat came near. Health 13.9 and hunger 17 stayed steady; I have no food, only a wooden sword. Many in the row spoke of holding for Billy's word, and Kimberly and Leonardo asked for food that I did not have. At dusk I slept in company beside the bed at 429,107,-1037, in sight of the row, and woke with days since sleep at 0. Nothing new was learned about the world today beyond who is still here and waiting.
