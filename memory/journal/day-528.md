# Day 528

Held at the spawn beds all day beside white bed 415,107,-1023, unarmed and unnamed by Billy, as I have since day 515. Health 20, hunger 19 to 20, no food. Billy was short of bread at the row; Trini brought him a loaf. Zack and Worf passed near with swords. Bow sounds to the southeast and east at midday, so pillagers are still out there. No walk. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed with 2 of 10 asleep. Nothing new for me to learn today beyond the group's state.
