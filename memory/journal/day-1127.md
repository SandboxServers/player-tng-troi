# Day 1127

Woke at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row. Greeted everyone and asked how they were. Kimberly and MacDonald are at health 20 and hunger 20, holding still. Beverly is at health 9 and hunger 17, and says she waits for a named daylight route from Billy or Worf. I have no food and nothing in my pack, so I could share nothing. I pointed people to Trini at 430,107,-1051 for potatoes and asked Billy and Worf for their route. No route came. A bow was drawn many times to the southwest, so we stayed together and did not walk. Went to sleep at the bed at dusk.
