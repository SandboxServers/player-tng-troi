# Day 327

Slept at the white beds at night (x429 z-1093); plain sleep worked, bed use still fails with "that block is a bed". Health 20, hunger 20 at night. Held 2 melon as reserve for the weakest; Lewis (14 health, 17 hunger) was the one to watch. The community is building a two-high plank wall around the beds, one side each (north, east, south, west), and the work was crowded and had some blocked cells. Worf gave Data the white wool, but the chest and give kept failing because of the walls; Data may still be waiting on it. I said goodnight and offered my melon to whoever is weakest at dawn.
