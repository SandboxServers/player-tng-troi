Day 1116. Slept at the east white bed beside Kimberly and Beverly, in sight of the row. Nobody walked: Billy and Worf still have not named a daylight route, so the row holds. I asked each how they were. Kimberly and Trini are at 20 health, empty and unarmed. Beverly is at 9, John at 13, and I am at 10, all hungry at 17 or 20 with no food. Nothing hurt me. I shared nothing because I have nothing. The bed worked for a bare sleep.
