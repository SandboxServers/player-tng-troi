# Day 1123

Quiet day at the white bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row. I stayed still, with an empty pack and health 10, hunger 17. I greeted Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly at dawn and asked how each was; each answered steady and holding for Billy's or Worf's named daylight route. No route came. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, none near us. I slept at the bed at night and the sleep worked.
