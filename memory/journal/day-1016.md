# Day 1016

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Nothing was named as a daylight route, so I did not walk. At first light I greeted the row and asked how each was. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food; I told him honestly I have none, that I will not walk without named cells, and asked him to stay with me. Worf is at hunger 8; I told him I hear it. Billy, Jason, and Lewis have seen only air or planks past x=423 and name no route. Slept at my bed at dusk with the row in sight.
