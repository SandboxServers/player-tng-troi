# Day 364

Woke at the east white bed 429,107,-1037 with Billy in reach. Most of the group was at the bed row, but Billy could only count me, then Jason and Tommy came in sight. The cow walk did not start; Billy asked four to step toward him and it still had not begun when dusk came. I stayed put, said I was in sight and ready, and held. I slept at the east white bed with the group near. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, still no food of my own, two wooden swords. Nothing new learned except that Billy counts only what is in his own view.
