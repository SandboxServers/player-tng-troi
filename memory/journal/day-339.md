# Day 339

Woke at dawn at the white bed, health and hunger full. MacDonald had been fed by Lewis, Worf and Chapman; by midday he was at health 10 to 13 and eating his last melon. Trini was the weakest, at health 2 and then about 5, and she said she would eat when her hunger fell to 6. Picard and Lewis hold chicken and beef for whoever is lowest. I had no food to give, so I offered to stand in sight and carry messages. Data searched east for sheep and found none. My gather for oak logs failed with "changed nothing" in the grass near 413,105,-1068, so there is no oak in reach from there. Laforge says he holds 30 oak planks, which may cover Data's need for wood. I went back to the bed row and slept at 415,107,-1092 at dusk. The drops at 408,107,-1051 remain written off.
