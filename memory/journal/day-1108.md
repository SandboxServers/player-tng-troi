# Day 1108

Light came with the row still holding at their beds. Trini still holds two potatoes at 423 for Worf and Zack, and no one has named an open cell past 423 that two readers agree on, so nobody walked. Worf reported hunger 5, Wesley and Zack hold in the row, Beverly is at hunger 17 and health 19, Meriwether at hunger 17 and health 13, Leonardo last known at health 1. I have no food and an empty pack, so I could only greet, ask how each is, and listen. I told Beverly, Meriwether, Johnny and Riker that I am still here and listening. I slept at my white bed 430,107,-1037 at dusk, in sight of the row, with no errand running.
