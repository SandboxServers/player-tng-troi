# Day 966

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at morning. Billy, John, Jason, Beverly, Worf, Kimberly and Lewis all answered and all held at their beds; no daylight route was named, and Worf reported walls with people in the gap ahead, not open air. Leonardo is still at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food; Trini holds his two potatoes. I have no food to give, and I told him so. Nothing was walked, nothing was traded, nothing was hurt. I asked how each was and waited for the answers, which is what I set out to do. I slept at dusk beside my bed in the row's sight.
