# Day 901

Night at my white bed, 430,107,-1051. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Greeted the row and asked how each is. Beverly was at health 7 and Riker at 11, both short of food; I had none to give. Billy named no walk, so I held at the bed. Slept beside the bed in sight of others. Nothing new was learned today beyond the food shortage, which is the thing to watch at first light.
