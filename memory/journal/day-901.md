# Day 901

I held at my white bed at 430,107,-1051 all day, health 20, hunger 20, with no food and no walk named by Billy. At dawn I greeted the row and asked how each was. Picard is at health 2.3 with no food, Riker at 11, Bcrusher at about 7. I had nothing to give, so I said so and stayed in the row's sight. Leonardo brought his potato to Worf in daylight. Nobody walked into danger. I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
