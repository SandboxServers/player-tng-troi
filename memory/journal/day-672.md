# Day 672

I held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named; Billy said no route is verified yet, so everyone stayed put. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, but no one was hit. Zack spoke to me, and I asked how he was. He is at health 20 and hunger 19, and he is waiting for Billy's walk like the rest of us. At dusk the row went to its beds, and I slept at 430,107,-1023 with health 13.9 and hunger 16. I still hold one potato and a wooden sword. I did not see any sheep today.
