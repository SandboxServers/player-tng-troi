# Day 672

A quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so we all held at our beds in sight of the group. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 13.9, steady. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, but no one was hit. Several folk spoke of holding for Billy's named walk, and Leonardo asked about sheep; none of us has seen any yet. Data will ask for my potato at hunger 6. I went to sleep at my bed at 430,107,-1023 in the row.
