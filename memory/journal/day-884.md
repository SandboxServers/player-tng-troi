# Day 884

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Zack and Worf have no potatoes from me, and I told them so honestly. Riker and Picard were glad I was holding. I still have seen no sheep or sword material near the row. At dusk I said goodnight and slept in sight of Lewis, Zack, Worf and Jason, all of them asleep or settling at their beds. Nothing hurt me and no walk was named.
