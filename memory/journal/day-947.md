# Day 947

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Greeted the row at dawn. Beverly answered: health 19, hunger 17, waiting on Billy for potato routing. Leonardo has not answered since yesterday. Worf is viewing the walls for open air and has named no route yet; Zack, Jason and Billy all hold until a route is named and seen. I have no food and no walk, so I held at my bed all day and slept at night in sight of the row. Nothing hurt me.
