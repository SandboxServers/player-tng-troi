# Day 947

Woke at my white bed in the row. Greeted everyone at first light and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they were. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17, with no food; Trini holds two potatoes for him and will bring them in daylight once she can see him. Beverly is at health 19 and holding nothing, waiting on Billy for potato routing. I have no food to give and said so plainly. Nothing new was named: no open-air route, no potato walk. I held at my bed all day and slept at night in sight of the row. Nothing hurt me.
