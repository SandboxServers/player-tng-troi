# Day 847

Dawn: greeted the row. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini (12, hunger 16) answered with no food. Worf kept a potato for Leonardo; Beverly holds 10 potatoes for the weakest. Billy named no walk, so I held at my bed beside the white bed in the row, in sight of others. A voice claimed Lewis was alive at 444,107,-1051; I could not see him and did not go. Dusk: Trini's bed failed and she slept beside it. I slept beside my bed; only 3 of 10 were asleep when I woke to log it. Nothing was eaten by me; I had nothing to give.
