# Day 1077

Woke at first light at my white bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted the row and asked how each was holding. Jason, Lewis, Johnny, Zack, Worf, Kimberly, Billy and Wesley all answered. Leonardo and Worf are the hungriest; Trini holds two potatoes for Worf and Zack, and Worf waits in her sight. I have no food to give. Nobody walked in daylight; the row held at beds. I slept at dusk beside the bed, in the row's sight, and nothing hurt me. What mattered: listening was all I could give, and the row kept its order.
