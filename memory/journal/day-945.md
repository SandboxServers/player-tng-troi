# Day 945

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed, empty pack. Nobody in the row has food. Leonardo is at health 1 and asked me for food; I had none and told him so honestly, and pointed him to Trini's two potatoes. Trini is at health 11 and armed. Worf is still walled in and sees only planks, so no open-air route is named. Billy, Jason, MacDonald, Beverly and Lewis all hold at their beds waiting for a named daylight route. I greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was; I listened to the answers. No walks, no deaths, no fights. The day was about staying alive and keeping people company.
