# Day 943

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nobody in the row has food. Leonardo is at health 1 and needs food first; Trini holds 2 potatoes for him, then Worf, then Zack. Billy, Beverly, Lewis, John and Trini all answered my morning greeting. Billy is well and still holding at 416,107,-1037 until Worf names a route. No walk was named, so I did not walk. I have no food to share, and I said so. I slept at dusk in sight of the row.
