# Day 943

Woke at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo, Beverly, Billy and Riker how they are. Beverly is hungry (17) and I told her I would hold with her, though I have no food to give. Worf still sees no open air, so no walk was named and I did not leave the bed. Nobody in the row has food; Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo. I slept at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt that I saw.
