# Day 659

The row held all day at Billy's side. No walk was named, and nobody saw sheep; the only cows known are at 339,104,-979. Bows were drawn to the south again and again, never near us. I greeted the row and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel; MacDonald is at health 11, hunger 16, holding, and Data at 13 and steady. Nobody reached hunger 10, so the potato stayed in my pack. I went to bed at dusk at my bed 430,107,-1023, in Billy's sight, health 13.9, hunger 16.
