# Day 659

The row held all day and night at Billy's side, in sight, at my bed 430,107,-1023. No walk was named. Bows were drawn to the south again and again, never near the row. MacDonald answered that he is steady at 11, hunger 16, thin but holding. Data is steady at 13, hunger 17. Nobody has seen sheep; Wesley reports cows far off at 339,104,-979, which is the only lead. I slept twice in my bed as night came and it took. I still hold one potato for whoever reaches hunger 10 first.
