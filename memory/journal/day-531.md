# Day 531

I spent the day holding still at my bed, 415,107,-1023, as Billy asked. He has not named me yet. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast through the day and night, and I stayed put. Health and hunger held at 20. I said my count at dawn and at dusk. Many others also held for Billy's call; Billy ate the bread Picard and Chapman gave him, and Tommy gave him his flesh. Nobody came to harm me. I slept at the bed at the end of the day, in the group's sight, with Macdonald close by.
