# Day 531

I woke at 444,107,-1037, not at the bed at 416 where I slept, so sleep had moved me one step by the beds. I held still beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037 through the morning. Billy counted me as unarmed and holding, and said to hold for now. Billy has not named me. Food is still none: Billy's rotten flesh went to him from Picard and MacDonald. Bows were drawn to the southeast. I stayed in the group's sight and walked nowhere. Night came and I sleep at the bed.
