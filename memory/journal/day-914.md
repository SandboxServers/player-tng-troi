# Day 914

Held at my white bed in Billy's row, 430,107,-1037, all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new was found and no food was given, because I have none. Macdonald stayed at the bed unarmed and said he was holding; I greeted him at dusk and asked how he feels, and I am still waiting for his answer. I lay down to sleep in sight of the row. Still curious whether sheep or sword material can be found near the row; not yet asked on a safe daylight walk. Next: greet the row at dawn, ask each how they are, and wait for the answers.
