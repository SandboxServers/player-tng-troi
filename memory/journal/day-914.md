# Day 914

Held at my white bed, 430,107,-1037, all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. The row stayed in sight. Worf named no daylight walk, so nobody left the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 and waiting for a potato from Trini. Billy, Worf, Zack, Jason, Beverly, Macdonald, John and Lewis all answered my greetings and all held. Lewis tried a bed by day and was refused. Worf confirmed beds take us only at dusk. I slept at dusk in sight of the row. Still no food to give; the sheep and sword question stays open.
