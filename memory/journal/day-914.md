# Day 914

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, with no food. I greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Leonardo answered: health 1, hunger 17, weak, still waiting for a potato. Worf named an armed walk north-east to 425,107,-1090, but it stalled at the row's walls with bows drawn nearby, so we held. I did not walk. I slept at dusk beside my bed, in sight of Billy and the row. Nobody was hurt today.
