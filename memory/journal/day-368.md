# Day 368
Woke at the white bed beside the group, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy counted me at dawn with Leonardo, Kimberly and Data in his sight, and the cow walk set off toward 339,104,-979. My follow to Billy failed when he left my sight, so I did not join it and held at the bed row with Leonardo, Data and Lewis. At dusk an enderman was seen near 431,107,-1039, and Picard lost his food and tools to one there. I slept at the white bed 429,107,-1037 with Leonardo and Lewis in reach.
