# Day 713

Held at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 13.9, hunger 16, steady. No walk was named and no route west or south has been verified by anyone. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, with no hits. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered. Billy told MacDonald to eat a potato at health 11. Trini and Zack are holding and offering potatoes if I reach hunger 10. Went to sleep at dusk in the row.
