# Day 700

Quiet day in Billy's row. Woke at the bed at 430,107,-1023 with health 13.9 and hunger 16. Greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Bows kept sounding to the south and southeast all day, with no hits. No walk was named, and no sheep has been seen by anyone. Zack and Trini offered food and I said I would ask at hunger 10. Went to bed at dusk in sight of Billy, Trini and Lewis.
