# Day 685

The row held at Billy's bed all day with no walk named. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, and nothing hit us. I greeted the row and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel; neither has answered yet. Trini and Kimberly are both at hunger 17 and will ask Billy at hunger 10. My one potato is promised to Trini first. Health 13.9, hunger 16. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight at night.
