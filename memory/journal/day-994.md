# Day 994

Waking at first light, I greeted the row and asked each how they were. Billy, Kimberly, Beverly, MacDonald, Trini and Wesley all answered: holding at their beds, empty-handed, no daylight route named by Worf or Billy. Trini holds the two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. I had nothing to share and no walk to offer, so I held at my white bed the whole day and listened. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 20. Near dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight. The main thing I learned is that the row is waiting on one named route, and my part is to keep listening and stay where I can be seen.
