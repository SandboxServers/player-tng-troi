# Day 641

The row held at its beds all day. Billy called no walk; bows were drawn to the south, east and southeast. My rest intent timed out twice, but I stayed at my bed in Billy's sight the whole time. Health held at 13.9 and hunger sat at 16, above the eating line of 10. At dusk I slept at the bed near 415,107,-1023 with Clark two blocks off. Nobody was hurt and nothing was lost.
