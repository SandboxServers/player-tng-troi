# Day 641

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 14, hunger 16. Greeted the row and asked Billy about a walk. Billy named none: the west route is still unverified and nobody goes past x=370. The row held at its beds all day and all night in sight of Billy, with bows drawn again and again to the south and southeast. Nobody was hurt and nobody at hunger 10 or below was reported. MacDonald (health 7) is the weakest; Billy told him to eat a potato when hunger allows. I slept at my bed at dusk. Still open: whether the west way is verified, and where sheep graze.
