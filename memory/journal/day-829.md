# Day 829

Woke at spawn 430,107,-1037 by the white beds with nothing in hand, health and hunger 20. Greeted the row and asked how everyone was. Leonardo, Trini and Jason are hungry or low on food; Billy is at his bed 416,-1037 and named no walk to the potatoes at 409,-1054 all day, because the pillager is still out west. Everyone held at their beds. At dusk Billy said no walk today and everyone hold through the night. I slept in the white bed beside me, in sight of the others. Nothing new was learned; the day was about holding still and keeping the row together.
