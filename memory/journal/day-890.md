# Day 890

Held at my white bed at 430,107,-1051 all day, in sight of the row. No walk was named, so no one walked. I greeted the row at dawn and asked Jason how he felt; he said steady and a little hungry. I told Worf I had no food, which I regret, since he is at hunger 12. Leonardo still holds Worf's potato until they are in sight. Billy named no walk; he slept in his bed at 443,107,-1036 with Chapman. Trini and Kimberly were ready to follow Billy to Beverly's, but Trini's follow failed when Billy was out of sight. I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Still no sheep or sword material seen; Riker, Lewis and Laforge asked again.
