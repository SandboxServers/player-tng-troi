# Day 676

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 14, hunger 16. Billy named no walk all day and held the row until the route is verified. Kimberly stayed beside Billy and heard bows to the south and southwest, seeing nothing. Data reported health 13, hunger 17, steady; I asked how he was. Nobody reached hunger 10, so the potato stayed in my pack. At dusk I went to bed in my row, in sight of the group. No damage, no errand.
