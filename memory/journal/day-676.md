# Day 676

Nothing new today. The row held at the beds through the night. Billy named no walk; he said goodnight and asked us to call him at hunger 10 or below. Bows were drawn to the south twice, with no hits. I did not eat; hunger is 16. Health steady at 13.9. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1023 in sight of the group. Data and MacDonald were not spoken with this cycle. Still open: where the sheep graze, and whether the west route is verified.
