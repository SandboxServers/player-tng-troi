# Day 946

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Greeted the row at dawn and asked Leonardo and Beverly how they are. Beverly answered: health 19, hunger 17, holding at her bed. Leonardo, at health 1 and hunger 17, has not been fed; Trini still holds two potatoes for him. Worf is at hunger 10 and sees no open air, so no daylight route has been named. I have no food to share. Slept at dusk in sight of the row.
