# Day 1037

Dusk at my white bed 430,107,-1037, in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted Kimberly, Johnny and Wesley; all are holding in place for the night and nobody has named food or an open daylight cell past 423. Kimberly is empty-handed too. I slept beside the bed, in the row's sight. Nothing new to walk toward.
