# Day 1037

Stayed at my white bed, 430,107,-1037, all day. Greeted the row at first light and asked how each was. Nobody has named an open cell past 423, so nobody walked. Leonardo is at health 1 and still has no food. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo and Worf and will bring one when Leonardo is in sight; Leonardo named his cell as 416,107,-1023. Worf is at hunger 8, Lewis at health 13, Zack at hunger 14. I had nothing to share. I told Leonardo, Lewis and Zack that I hear them. I slept beside my bed at dusk in the row's sight.
