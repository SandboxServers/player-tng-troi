# Day 838

Woke at spawn beside the white beds in Billy's row. Greeted the row at dawn and asked how each was. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) and Trini (health 12, hunger 16) still have no food; Trini has only seeds. I had none to give. No walk was named by Billy, so I held at the beds in sight of the row all day, and slept beside my bed at dusk. Nothing was hurt, nothing was lost.
