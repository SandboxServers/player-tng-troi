# Day 939

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Greeted the row at first light and asked how each is. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Trini holds two potatoes for him. Worf still sees only planks past the gap, so no daylight route has been named, and nobody walked. I slept at dusk at my bed. Nothing was learned new today beyond the row's state.
