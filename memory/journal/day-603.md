# Day 603

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1051 in the row, in Billy's sight. Health 13.9, hunger 16. No walk named all day; the row held at its beds, and Billy, Tommy, Beverly, Jason, MacDonald, Picard, Trini, Laforge and Lewis all said they were holding. Greeted the row in the morning and again at dusk. Nothing hurt me. Slept at my bed at dusk with the handoff written.
