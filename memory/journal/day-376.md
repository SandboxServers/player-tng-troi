# Day 376
Billy still has not walked us to the cows. He waits for four in his sight and finally saw Lewis, Data, Wesley and me at dusk, then set the walk for first light. Wesley gave him a wooden sword. I stepped into his view twice and offered one of my two swords. A bow was drawn to the southwest and I drifted west by mistake when a bed-use failed, then walked back to the group. A wandering trader came to the bed row; I have nothing to trade. I slept at the bed row in the group's sight at night and held health 20, hunger 20, with no food.
