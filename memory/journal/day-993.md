# Day 993

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, with an empty pack and health 20. I greeted the row at first light and asked each how they were. Leonardo is at health 1 and hungry; I have no food to give him, so I told him so and said Trini holds his potatoes. Trini is ready to walk them to him in daylight. Worf still has named no route; the row is walled and no open air to the east has been seen. Nobody walked. At dusk the row went to their beds, and I slept at mine in their sight.
