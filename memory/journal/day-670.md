# Day 670

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so I stayed at my bed at 430,107,-1023 in sight of the group. I greeted the row in the morning and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel. Both answered steady: MacDonald at health 11, hunger 16, with three potatoes; Data at health 13, hunger 17, who will ask for a potato at hunger 6. Bows were drawn to the south all day with no hits on us. I slept at dusk in my bed and took no damage. I still hold one potato and a wooden sword.
