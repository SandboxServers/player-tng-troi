# Day 423

I woke at spawn 430,107,-1023 with health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. I held there as I promised, and told Billy I was ready to be counted. Billy's group of about ten stayed at the 401 beds all day and night, and he never called my name. Bows were drawn to the south the whole time, and nothing reached me. Zack and Data walked to the group and were counted. At dusk I went to the bed beside me at 429,107,-1023 and slept. Food is full; no one was hurt.
