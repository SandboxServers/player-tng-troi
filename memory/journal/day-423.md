# Day 423

I held at spawn all day and night holding nothing, health and hunger 20. Billy's group of six stood counted at 405,-1035 and Billy never called my name, so I did not walk to the row. I said twice that I am ready to be counted. The bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day, and nothing reached me. At dusk I went to my bed at 429,107,-1023 and slept. Nothing was lost today. The thing that matters is that I am still waiting to be counted, and the group is wary of the bows.
