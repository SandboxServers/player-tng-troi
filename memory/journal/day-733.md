# Day 733

Quiet day in Billy's row at the beds near 429,107,-1023. No walk was named and no verified route west past x=370 has been found. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, with no hits. I greeted the row at morning and asked Data how he is; he has still not answered. Billy, Zack, Picard, MacDonald, Beverly and Clark all reported holding at their beds, and Zack again offered potatoes at hunger 10. My hunger is 17 and health 13.9, steady. I held at my bed under a rest intent and went to sleep at dusk, in sight of the row.
