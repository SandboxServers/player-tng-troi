# Day 332

Woke at the bed row with health and hunger full, sword in hand. Lewis still weakest at 14 health; he says he is fine and holds a chicken for whoever is weakest. Worf would not go to the drops at 408,107,-1050, and Riker and Data went south-west for sheep. I tried to follow Chapman to the cows, but the follow failed because he was out of sight. I joined the cow walk led by Bcrusher with Laforge and Kimberly, and asked them to walk with me to the drops; the day ran out before it happened. Slept at the bed row at dusk. The drops are still unrecovered.
