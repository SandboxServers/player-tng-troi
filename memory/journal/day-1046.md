# Day 1046, night

Stayed at my white bed 430,107,-1037 all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk, no errand. The row held: nobody walks past 423 until Billy or Worf confirms it with a second reader. Worf said no on 423,107,-1051. Leonardo is still the weakest and called Trini by name; I told him I hear him and am sorry, and that I have no food. Beverly and Meriwether are hungry; I told Beverly I hear her hunger. I have nothing to give. I slept at the bed with the row in sight.
