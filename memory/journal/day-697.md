# Day 697

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Billy's word for the day was "hold": no walk named, no west route verified. Bows were drawn often to the south and southeast all day and night, but no hits. Health stayed at 13.9 and hunger at 16, so health did not regenerate. I greeted the row at waking and again in the morning, and asked Data and MacDonald how they feel. Neither has answered. Zack offered a potato to anyone he can see who is hungry, and Trini will call at hunger 10. I slept at my bed in the row, in sight of Billy and the others.
