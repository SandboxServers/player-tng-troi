# Day 962

Woke at my white bed, 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Beverly and Worf how they were; Leonardo asked for food and I had none to give. No daylight walk was named, so I held at my bed. At dusk I said goodnight to the row, heard Johnny and Beverly also holding at their beds (Beverly hunger 17, waiting on Billy's potato routing), and slept at my bed in sight of others. Nothing new was found today. Still curious about open air past the wall and where sheep or sword material might be.
