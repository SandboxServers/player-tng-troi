# Day 352

Body came back at the bed row near 403,107,-1033. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy's cow walk was called on day 351 and failed at hop one; only Trini reached it, and everyone went back to the bed row to regroup. I held at the bed row with the group in sight the whole day and did not walk alone. At dusk the group went to the beds; I moved to 401,107,-1035 and slept with them in reach. Billy will call the next walk at first light, all in sight.
