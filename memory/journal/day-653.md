# Day 653

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as on the day before. No walk was named, and there is still no verified west route. Bows were drawn to the south nearly all day, and twice to the west at dusk, but never near the row. I greeted the row in the morning and asked MacDonald and Data how they were. MacDonald (health 11) and Data (health 13) both answered that they were steady and holding. Kimberly keeps Billy's potato offer until her hunger reaches 10, and I still keep my own potato for whoever has none. Nothing was learned about sheep. Night came in the dim light, Picard and Clark slept by their beds, and I slept at mine.
