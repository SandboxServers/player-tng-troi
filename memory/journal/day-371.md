# Day 371

Woke at the bed row near 443,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20, still no food. Told the group I was in sight and ready for Billy's cow walk; Lewis and Data said the same. Billy did not call the walk. Picard reported he was at the bed row. Night came, and I slept in the white bed beside me; the sleep only reached 5 of 10 needed, so it was a short rest. Nothing hurt me and no enderman was seen.
