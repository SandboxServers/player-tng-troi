# Day 581

Woke off my bed at 415,107,-1021 and walked back to 429,107,-1023 under travel. Held at the row with Billy in reach through the whole day while bows were drawn to the south and southeast; nobody was hit. Billy gave me 2 potatoes at health 8. I ate 3 and health rose to about 12 by night. No walk was named. Slept at night beside my bed with Billy in the row's sight.
