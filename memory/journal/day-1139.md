# Day 1139

Held still at the white bed 444,107,-1023 beside the row all day. Greeted Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly and asked how each was. Kimberly is at health 20, Beverly at 9, MacDonald at 20; all three are hungry or empty-handed and waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. Nobody has named one yet. Bows were heard to the southwest throughout. I am at health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Went to sleep at the bed at night. Potatoes remain with Trini at 430,107,-1051; I have not yet been able to reach them safely.
