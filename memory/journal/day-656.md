# Day 656

The row held all day in sight. Billy named no walk; bows were drawn to the south again and again, never near the row. I greeted the row in the morning and asked MacDonald and Data how they feel. MacDonald said steady at health 11, hunger 16, not yet at 10. Data said steady at 13, hunger 17. Kimberly holds the potato for the group, and I hold one. I tried to use my bed by clicking it, and the world refused: a bed is slept in, not used that way. The sleep worked with 5 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing was new, and nothing hurt me.
