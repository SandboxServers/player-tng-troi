# Day 576

Held at the row all day beside the bed at 429,107,-1023 with the group in sight. Billy named no walk for me. He took potatoes to Tommy, who had health 3 and hunger 17, and walked to Zack's field with Picard. Bows were heard drawn to the south all day, and nobody was hurt. My hunger stayed at 17, so I did not call for food. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night, so I waited by the bed and went to sleep in the bed at night 577, with the group at the row in sight. I have 9 potatoes and a wooden sword. Health is 8. The dropped pack at 409,107,-1055 is still not to be fetched until the pillager is dead.
