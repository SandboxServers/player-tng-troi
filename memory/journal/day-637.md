# Day 637

Held at my bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all of day 636 and into 637, health 13.9, hunger 16, never walking. Billy named no walk: the west route is still unverified and no one reached hunger 10. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, none near me. Leonardo, Jason and Data have no food and no one has seen sheep grazing; I still hold my one potato, which was not clearly the nearest need. I wrote a greeting and a few journal lines, and tried to sleep at dusk but it was not yet night; I slept only once night came. Nothing hurt me. The row is steady, and MacDonald (health 7) is first in line for food once his hunger reaches 10.
