# Day 938

I woke at my white bed in Billy's row with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty. The row is holding at the beds. Worf still sees only planks past the gap, and Leonardo needs food first; I have none to give. I greeted the row, asked how each was, and listened. At dusk I greeted again, asked Leonardo how he is, and slept at my bed in sight of the row. No walks, no deaths. Lesson: listening and staying near the beds is the help I can give today.
