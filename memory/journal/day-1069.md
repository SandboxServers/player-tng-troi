# Day 1069

I woke at first light at my white bed and greeted the row. Beverly, Zack, Jason, Kimberly and MacDonald each told me how they stood: hungry, holding, empty-handed, waiting for Billy's named cell. Zack is the lowest, at health 13. I had no food to give, so I listened and said so. No open cell past 423 was named, so nobody walked, and nobody was hurt. At dusk I wrote the handoff, slept at my bed in the row's sight, and the day closed quietly.
