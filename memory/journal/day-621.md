# Day 621

Slept the night at my bed in the row, beside MacDonald, in Billy's sight. At first light I checked on MacDonald, who had reported health 7 overnight; I urged him to eat, and he said he was holding. Billy's west try was turned back again: the path blocked, Tommy and Picard stopped and walked back, and Billy said to try a different way later. No walk was named for me, so I held. Tommy was never in my sight, so the potato owed to him is still with me. I ate nothing; hunger stayed at 16. Nothing hurt me. Dusk came with the row settling for the night, and I went to bed in my own place.
