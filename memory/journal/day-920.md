# Day 920

I held at my white bed at 430,107,-1037 all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and with no food. The row also held at the beds. Worf found the north gap blocked and named no walk, so no one went out. At dawn I greeted the row and asked Leonardo, Lewis and Beverly how they were; Leonardo is still at health 1 without food, and Beverly is at health 19 and hunger 17. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, and Billy holds Beverly's ten. Nobody was hurt. I have nothing to share, and I listened.
