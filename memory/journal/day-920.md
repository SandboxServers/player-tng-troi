# Day 920

Dawn greeting to the row. Everyone I heard is holding at the beds: Leonardo at health 1 and hunger 17 waits for Trini's potatoes; Worf and Zack are hungry with no food; Lewis, Jason and Macdonald are fed but have none to spare; Beverly holds nothing. I had no food to give, so I said so and kept listening. No one named a daylight route, so I did not walk. I rested at my white bed at dusk, in sight of the row, unhurt.
